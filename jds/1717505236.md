About the role and team . We're looking for current students to join Uber's Product Management team in San Francisco this summer for 12 weeks. This is an internship designed for curious, analytical students who want to learn how products get built, shipped, and measured at Uber's scale. . As a Product Management Intern, you'll be embedded within a product team, working with a manager and a dedicated PM mentor to own a scoped product problem end to end. Your deliverable will be a product recommendation—or a shipped improvement—that supports one of our key product priorities and has broader company impact. We hope you'll envision a dynamic summer with our team—and potentially beyond. . . What you’ll do . Own a real product problem from start to finish: define the problem, size the opportunity, propose a solution, and drive it toward launch with your manager and mentor . Use quantitative and qualitative insights—data analysis, experiments, and conversations with riders, drivers, couriers, eaters, and merchants—to understand customer needs and pain points . Write crisp product documents—problem statements, requirements, and success metrics—and use them to align partners . Partner with Engineering, Design, Data Science, Operations, and Marketing to prioritize and deliver . Present your insights, tradeoffs, and recommendations to Product leadership at the end of the summer . . Basic qualifications .
