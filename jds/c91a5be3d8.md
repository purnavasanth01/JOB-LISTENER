## Job description (verbatim)

Camping World is hiring a BI & AI Analyst Intern to measure company-wide adoption of AI tools and performance of AI agents. The intern will prepare usage data, build dashboards, analyze business value, and present findings to stakeholders and leadership.. . Responsibilities. Write and troubleshoot SQL in Snowflake to pull, clean, and validate AI usage and agent data. Work with our AI engineers to understand what usage and log data each tool or agent produces and where it is stored. Help define adoption and usage KPIs and document how each one is calculated. Build and maintain Data Analytics dashboards, and review drafts with business users to get their feedback. Look for adoption trends, such as which teams use AI tools, which teams do not, and where usage drops off, and share what you find. Support ROI and business value analysis for AI tools and agents with help from senior team members. Write short summaries and presentations of your analysis for business stakeholders. Find and document data quality issues and escalate them when needed. Use approved AI tools for your own analysis, documentation, and SQL. . Skills. Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Business, Data Analytics, Information Systems, Computer Science, Statistics, or a related field. Working knowledge of SQL from coursework, projects, or prior internships. Basic familiarity with a BI tool or willingness to learn quickly. Careful with details and checks work before sharing it. Interest in AI tools and how companies adopt them.
