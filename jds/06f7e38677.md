Vatic Outsourcing is hiring a Business Intelligence Analyst Intern for its Business Intelligence team. The intern will develop dashboards and trackers, manage and update data, support internal teams, and communicate with telecom carriers while gaining experience in the telecom industry.. . Building dashboards and invoice/service trackers in Microsoft Excel to quantify device usage and billing metrics, device inventory, user lists, and an overall ROI for all Vatic services to be presented to the client. Assisting account managers and analysts with various additional projects. . Skills. Intermediate proficiency in Microsoft Excel (XLOOKUP, Pivot Tables). Strong initiative, analytical skills, attention to detail, etc. Comfortable communicating with clients and vendors. Experience with Microsoft Power BI is a plus, but willingness to learn is required. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel (PowerQuery, VBA Macros). Previous experience in a customer service or data-related role. . Benefits. Develop proficiency with Microsoft Excel and PowerBI and gain knowledge of the telecom industry.. Valuable hands-on experience..
