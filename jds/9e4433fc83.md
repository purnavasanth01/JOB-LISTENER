## Job description (verbatim)

Teradata is seeking an AI Studio Product Manager Intern to shape the roadmap and launch plan for an AI Studio feature area. The intern will translate customer and field input into product requirements, track adoption, conduct competitive analysis, and present findings and recommendations to product leadership.. . Responsibilities. Interview customers and field team members. Turn the notes into written problem statements and ranked requirements. Write a product requirements document for one AI studio app feature, with acceptance criteria engineering can test against. Send weekly roadmap updates for your feature area and flag dependencies with other product areas. Complete one competitive analysis of AI studio app offerings, covering pricing, feature gaps, and positioning. Use it as input to your requirements. Track adoption and usage metrics for a launched feature, build a weekly dashboard, and recommend one change based on the data. Draft the launch plan and release notes for your feature and agree on timing with Marketing and Sales Enablement. Summarize beta-customer feedback and triage incoming requests into the backlog with the engineering manager. . Skills. Currently enrolled in a bachelor's or master's program in business, computer science, information systems, engineering, or a related field, with at least one academic term remaining after the internship. Coursework, a club role, or a project where you defined requirements, ranked features, or tracked a team's deliverables. Comfort reading query results or spreadsheet data to answer a product question. Ability to write clear one- to two-page documents in English. Curiosity about why customers pick one platform over another, and the habit of asking follow-up questions until the problem is clear. Judgment on tradeoffs, including when a feature is worth building now and when it can wait. Willingness to work with engineers who push back on scope, and to change a requirement when the evidence says so. Ability to explain a technical concept to a sales rep or customer in plain language. Follow-through on action items across several stakeholders. Interest in how enterprise buyers decide on platforms, including security, governance, and cost. Familiarity with cloud data platforms, or with AI tools such as LLM chat assistants or APIs you use in daily work. Experience with a product analytics tool or dashboard software. Prior internship or project work in product management, marketing analytics, or customer research. . Benefits. A named mentor on the product management team, with weekly one-on-ones..
