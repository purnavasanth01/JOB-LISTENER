## Job description (verbatim)

Dimensional Innovations (DI) is hiring a UI/UX Design Intern to help design intuitive digital experiences for client projects. The intern will work with the Creative Studio and technology, software development, and engineering teams on user research, interface design, and interactive prototypes.. . Responsibilities. Contribute UI/UX design thinking to real DI client projects. Help develop user experiences for interactive installations and digital experiences. Create user flows, journey maps, wireframes and interactive prototypes using Figma. Explore concepts through research, sketching, visual storytelling and rapid iteration. Develop detailed user interface designs that balance usability, visual design and the overall experience. Help communicate concepts through compelling visual storytelling and project presentations. Participate in user-centered design activities and discussions. Gather inspiration and build visual vocabularies to help guide creative direction. Conduct market, audience and design research to inform project decisions. Contribute to conceptual design, design detailing and branding as appropriate. Collaborate closely with designers, software developers, Creative Technology and engineering teams. Participate in critiques, project meetings and design reviews. Stay curious about emerging trends, technology, culture and how people interact with digital experiences. . Skills. A strong interest in UI/UX, interaction design and user-centered design. Experience using Figma for interface design, wireframing or prototyping. Strong visual design and visual storytelling skills. Familiarity with Adobe Creative Suite, including Illustrator, InDesign and Photoshop. An interest in user research, journey mapping and understanding how people interact with technology. Strong conceptual thinking and creative problem-solving skills. Curiosity about emerging technology, design trends and popular culture. A collaborative mindset and interest in working with both creative and technical teams. The ability to adapt and "go where needed" in a fast-moving creative environment. Comfort experimenting, receiving feedback, iterating and trying again. Strong communication, organization and attention to detail. Students pursuing UI/UX Design, Interaction Design, Graphic Design, Industrial Design or a related field are encouraged to apply. A portfolio is required to be considered for the UI/UX Design Internship. Experience with motion, 3D design or tools such as After Effects or SketchUp is a plus. . Benefits. Contribute to actual client projects—not just hypothetical intern assignments.. Exposure to the design process from early UX thinking and interface design through development, testing and ultimately something people can actually interact with.. Collaborate closely with Creative Technology and software development teams, gaining firsthand experience designing alongside the people responsible for bringing your interfaces to life.. Exposure to different audiences, environments and design challenges across sports, museums, healthcare, entertainment and other markets.. Work alongside designers, developers, engineers, project managers and fabricators, gaining exposure to disciplines you may not experience in the classroom.. Built-in mentorship: experienced creative and technical professionals provide feedback, challenge your thinking and help you continue developing your skills.. Employee-owned ESOP company, where employees share in the success of the company.. Opportunities to connect with fellow interns and DI employees through intern events, activities and experiences throughout the summer.. . Company Overview.
