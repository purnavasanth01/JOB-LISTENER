Constellation Brands is seeking a Data and AI Enablement Intern to support adoption of modern data, analytics, and AI capabilities across the enterprise. The intern will work with the Data & Analytics Platform team on Microsoft Fabric initiatives, data pipelines and models, Power BI experiences, and generative AI capabilities.. . Responsibilities. Work with technical mentors on strategic Data & Analytics Platform initiatives supporting Microsoft Fabric and enterprise AI enablement. Assist with the development and testing of Microsoft Fabric Lakehouses, including organizing data within OneLake and working with Delta-based data structures. Build and enhance Fabric Gen2 Dataflows and Fabric Data Pipelines to ingest, transform, and orchestrate data from enterprise data sources. Assist with designing and maintaining Power BI semantic models, including relationships, measures, business definitions, and reusable analytics components. Develop and enhance Power BI reports and dashboards that provide intuitive, actionable insights to business users. Explore and test Microsoft Fabric Copilot capabilities across data engineering, analytics, semantic modeling, and Power BI scenarios. Assist with devleloping Fabric Data Agents that enable users to interact with governed enterprise data through natural-language questions. Provide ad hoc data analysis, technical documentation, presentation, and project support as needed. Participate in Constellation Brands internship activities, including senior leader meetings, goal setting, networking, team-building activities, tours, and a final internship presentation, consistent with the existing internship program structure. .
