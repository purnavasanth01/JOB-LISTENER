The Role The Sun Life U.S. DentaQuest Claims Technology team is looking for a proactive, positive, and dynamic team player to join our team to design and code innovative automation solutions to help our team focus less on administrative tasks and more on human-powered decision making. In this role you will gain valuable, real-world experience working with both business operations and IT colleagues, allowing you opportunity to learn where technology and operations intersect to produce business value. You will host meetings, do opportunity analysis, prioritize and implement code-based automations that will help our operations team run more efficiently and with higher quality outcomes. This is an excellent opportunity to get hands-on experience and see immediate impact. Responsibilities will include, but are not limited to: Regularly meets with operations colleagues to identify and design automations Develops business requirements for proposed automations and evaluate the cost-benefit between them ultimately decide which ones to prioritize Implements prioritized automations using object-oriented programming techniques Carefully and thoroughly tests / deploys automations Monitors and manages execution of the automations and resolves issues as they arise Drafts and sends communications to the team highlighting new automations and their value to the team Performs other duties as assigned Core skill sets needed for this role: Experience with object-oriented design and programming (Microsoft C# preferred) Experience with IDE development tools (Microsoft Visual Studio or Code preferred) Analytical skills Ability to set up, conduct and manage effective meetings Working knowledge of MS office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint Outlook, etc…) Excellent verbal written communication skills Proactive, able to manage multiple projects with demanding deadlines Highly motivated, self-driven, focused, strong attention to detail The Candidate To be eligible for a co-op at Sun Life, you must meet the following requirements: Currently enrolled in an accredited college or university during the time of the co-op (January 2027 – June 2027) Must be an undergraduate or graduate student in a related field of study Eligible to legally work in the United States Ability to work full-time (40 hours/week) during co-op session Compensation for this role will be around $22 an hour.
