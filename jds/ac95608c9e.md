## Job description (verbatim)

Paylocity is hiring an Implementation Software Engineering Intern to build and support internal tools, integrations, and automations for its Implementation teams. The intern will work with end users and subject-matter experts to understand business needs, develop and test practical software solutions, and gather feedback to improve them.. . Responsibilities. Partner directly with Implementation end users and subject-matter experts to understand their workflows, pain points, and goals; ask clarifying questions and document business requirements before building. Translate business needs into clear problem statements, user stories, acceptance criteria, and simple technical plans, and confirm understanding with stakeholders before and during development. Design, build, and maintain internal software tools, scripts, and automated workflows that reduce manual effort for Implementation teams, with guidance and mentorship from experienced engineers. Use AI-assisted development tools to learn new technologies, explore existing code, prototype ideas quickly, and deliver working solutions without sacrificing quality. Write clean, simple, and readable code, and test your work, including edge cases and error conditions, before releasing it to users. Use version control and collaborative development workflows; participate in code reviews and incorporate feedback to improve code quality. Track and work through assigned bugs and enhancement requests in a structured Jira backlog, and communicate progress, blockers, and tradeoffs clearly. Gather end-user feedback after delivery, support user acceptance testing, and iterate on solutions based on what you learn. Document solutions, decisions, and user guidance so tools can be understood, supported, and adopted by others. Participate in team meetings to discuss priorities, share progress, and collaborate on technical approaches. . Skills.
