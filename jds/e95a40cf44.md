## Job description (verbatim)

Note: The job is a remote job and is open to candidates in USA. Residex builds software for senior living operators and caregivers. The UX Design Intern will help develop a design system, data visualization patterns, and AI-enabled UX tools that support accessible, consistent design across the platform, collaborating with senior designers, product managers, and engineers.. . Responsibilities. Help structure our design system's tokens, components and documentation so AI tools can read and apply them correctly. Contribute to our design language or our data visualization patterns (charts, tables, dashboards), depending on your focus, so they stay consistent and accessible across modules. Help build UX harnesses and tooling: the context, rules, checks and plugins that keep AI-generated work consistent with our design system, accessible and on-brand. Prototype agents that bring design best practices to engineering and product teams, such as a review agent that checks a screen or chart against our standards before it ships. Help product managers and engineers prototype with the design system and AI, and learn where they get stuck. Draft guidance on when AI output is ready to use and when it needs a designer, for senior designers to review. Present your work in critique and give a final readout to design, product and engineering leadership. . Skills. Current undergraduate or graduate students in design, UX, HCI, informatics, data visualization, creative technologies, communication design or a related field. A portfolio (link, or PDF) with two to four projects. At least one should show your focus: design system or design language work (a component set, type and color system, or brand language), or data visualization and tooling work (charts, dashboards, a data-heavy interface, or a tool or plugin you built). Ideally one was built with or for AI. . Benefits. Remote first. . Company Overview. Residex® delivers Intelligent Care through a secure, all-in-one assisted living software platform that empowers caregivers with data-driven tools for smarter, safer, and more efficient care. It was founded in 2000, and is headquartered in Greenwood Village, Colorado, USA, with a workforce of 51-200 employees. Its website is https://www.residex.ai.. .
