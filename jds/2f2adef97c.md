## Job description (verbatim)

About the Role . . Turn your classroom learning into real-world impact with H&R Block’s Embark 2027 Summer Internship Program, open to students studying Computer Science and Software Engineering . This 11-week paid internship offers meaningful, real-world experience that aligns with your field of study while making a valuable impact on our organization. . . The Embark 2027 Summer Internship runs from May 17, 2027 – July 30, 2027. . . You’ll work at our corporate headquarters in the heart of downtown Kansas City’s Power & Light District, where you’re just steps, or a streetcar ride, away from incredible restaurants, entertainment, and city energy. Inside our building, you’ll have access to amenities that make work-life balance easy, including an onsite Starbucks, a company dining hall with plenty of options, a free fitness center, and free parking! . Program Highlights: . $25/hour compensation . Paid PTO during H&R Block’s annual Reboot Week (June 28 – July 2) . Housing stipend provided for students whose permanent address is more than 50 miles from the KC metro . . Throughout the 11-week internship, under the guidance of a leader, some of the internship responsibilities will include: . Research, gather and organize data to provide information for special projects or reports. . Work closely with the QA, User Experience and other teams to ensure consistent understanding of requirements and approach. . Write, test, debug, and maintain code that supports real products and customer experiences . Investigate technical problems and collaborate with team members to identify and implement solutions . Participate in Agile team activities, including daily stand-ups, sprint planning, reviews, and other team ceremonies . Participate in code reviews and incorporate feedback to strengthen code quality and development practices . Learn existing applications, systems, architecture, and development practices while contributing to an established codebase . Design, develop and maintain software components and products that support H&R Block business applications. . Use tools, languages, coding standards and best practices to develop new product features and maintain year over year product functionality and enhancements. . Work alongside experienced engineers while receiving ongoing coaching, feedback, and opportunities for technical development . Interns will be provided with significant guidance and mentoring but will also be encouraged to work independently as well. . . Throughout the 11-week internship, as a part of the Embark program, interns will also participate in workshops and events that align with the program's four pillars: . . Professional Development : includes the basics for a professional role, everything from using Microsoft Office to setting up a Teams meeting and giving a presentation. . . Giveback : involves participating in Make Every Block Better community service events. The goal is to teach the importance of community, make an impact, and provide bonding time for teams. . . Networking : entails meeting various executives with offices at H&R Block’s headquarters, having lunch with directors and vice presidents, spending time with a mentor and getting to know people beyond their assigned department. . . Connected Culture : is meant to be fun and teach the importance of working hard and playing hard. H&R Block might take the interns to a Kansas City Royals game or facilitate other competitions meant to be fun or instill teamwork. . . Required Qualifications . . Education : . High school diploma or equivalent . Seeking to obtain a degree in Information Technology , Computer Science , Information Science , Software Engineering, or some similar discipline with a GPA of 3.0 or above . Ability to work as part of a diverse team and independently . Highly motivated, organized, reliable, and detail-oriented . Strong written and verbal communication skills . Quick learner and not afraid to work with new computer-based programs and databases . Ability to be effective and contribute in a fast-paced corporate environment . Work Experience: . No previous work experience necessary . . Preferred Qualifications . . . . . Foundational knowledge of software development concepts, object-oriented programming, and the software development lifecycle (SDLC) . Experience with or exposure to one or more programming languages, such as C#, .NET, Python, Java, JavaScript, or similar technologies . Familiarity with cloud technologies and development environments, particularly Microsoft Azure . Exposure to web services, APIs, microservices, event-driven architecture, or cloud-native application development . Familiarity with relational and/or NoSQL databases, such as SQL Server or Cosmos DB . Interest in mobile application development and native mobile experiences . Familiarity with source control, CI/CD, or development collaboration tools such as Git and Azure DevOps . Interest in AI-first software engineering, including the responsible use of AI-assisted development tools .
