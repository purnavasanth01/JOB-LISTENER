## Job description (verbatim)

Carlisle Construction Materials is seeking a Product Management Intern to analyze product history, markets, competitive positioning, and product specifications across multiple product lines. The intern will build reusable models and reporting tools, summarize findings, and present recommendations while coordinating with Product Management, Marketing, Technical Services, and Sales.. . Responsibilities. Compile multi-year product history from internal systems, exports, and spreadsheets into consolidated working data sets. Document how products have been positioned, specified, and applied over time, and identify shifts in usage. Establish a consistent profile format that can be applied across multiple product lines. Research the markets served by each product line, including size, growth drivers, and customer segments. Assess competitive alternatives and identify where CCM products are strongly or weakly positioned. Summarize market trends and outside factors that influence product demand. Build scenario-based models that show how portfolio changes could affect volume, mix, and customer impact. Test assumptions and sensitivities, and clearly document the logic behind each model. Design the models to be reusable so they can be applied to additional product lines. Review applicable code bodies and specification listings for completeness and accuracy. Document gaps, inconsistencies, and obsolete entries, and recommend corrections. Develop Excel models, pivot tables, and dashboards that make the analysis usable for ongoing decision-making. Create a repeatable process and documentation so the work can be refreshed after the internship ends. Prepare written summaries and presentation materials for Product Management and Marketing leadership. Present final conclusions and recommendations to the project team at the close of the internship. Work with Product Management, Marketing, Technical Services, and Sales to validate data and resolve open questions. . Skills. Strong Microsoft Excel skills required, including pivot tables, lookups, and building models with large data sets. Analytical mindset with strong attention to detail and accuracy. Proficiency in Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, and Outlook. Excellent written and verbal communication skills; able to explain findings clearly to a non-technical audience.
