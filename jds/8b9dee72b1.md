We're looking for a Co-op to join our research team and explore how AI agents can support engineers working on active sound management technologies.. The role sits at the boundary between signal processing and software engineering. You don't need to be an active noise control expert on day one; you will learn the domain from senior engineers who build these tools. You should be a strong programmer who is comfortable reading engineering code and asking why it works.. In this role, you will:. Capture expert knowledge. Work with senior engineers to document engineering workflows: key decisions, heuristics, failure modes, and what a finished result looks like.. Modernize engineering tools. Refactor existing tools into modular functions with well-defined inputs and outputs.. Connect tools to AI agents. Make engineering functions available to LLM-based agents using Python and modern tool-calling interfaces.. Measure what works. Build an evaluation framework that compares agent results against expert-produced references.. Build in safeguards. Implement automated checks that keep agent outputs within engineering and safety limits.. Prototype and demonstrate. Build an agent-assisted workflow and demonstrate it on a real engineering use case.. Report and document. Present your results and document your work so the team can build on it after your term.. . To excel in this role, candidates should possess the following:. Currently pursuing a PhD or MS in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Mechanical Engineering, or a related field. Strong Python: writing modular code, packaging functions, using git, and writing tests. Working knowledge of MATLAB, enough to read and refactor existing engineering scripts. Signal processing fundamentals: FFT, spectra, transfer functions, coherence, and digital filters.
