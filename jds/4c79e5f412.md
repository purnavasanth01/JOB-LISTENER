## Job description (verbatim)

Zillow is hiring a Product Design Intern to contribute to a scoped design project, from defining customer problems to exploring, prototyping, and refining solutions. The intern will collaborate across teams, apply user-centered and accessible design practices, and present their process and recommendations.. . Responsibilities. Contribute to a scoped design project: With guidance from experienced designers, help define a customer problem, explore solutions, and develop user flows, wireframes, interactive prototypes, and refined interface designs. Present your recommendations, process, and learnings at the end of the internship. Collaborate cross-functionally: Partner with designers, product managers, engineers, and research or content partners. Learn how teams evaluate ideas, define success criteria, and balance customer needs with technical constraints. Document design intent and support implementation review. Simplify workflows and apply design systems: Help make customer tasks easier to complete by improving navigation, interactions, and information clarity. Use established design system components and patterns, considering accessibility and consistency throughout your work. Practice user-centered design and iteration: Review existing research and customer feedback, support usability testing, and translate findings into design improvements with guidance. Share work in design critiques, explain your thinking, and refine solutions based on feedback. Learn how to incorporate AI tools into your design work. With the changing landscape of tools that designers use, it’s important to learn how to use them to your advantage. You’ll work side-by-side with experienced designers who work with AI tools daily. These tools are evolving quickly, and the hands-on experience will be invaluable. . Skills. Pursuing a Bachelors or Masters degree in design, human-computer interaction, or a related field. Share projects that demonstrate foundational UX/UI skills, user-centered problem solving, and your design process. Coursework, personal projects, and volunteer work are welcome; explain the problem, your role, your design decisions, and what you learned. Foundational understanding of interaction design, user flows, wireframes, and prototyping, with experience using Figma or a comparable design tool. Coursework and personal projects count; prior professional product design experience is not required. Interest in understanding customer needs, using research to inform decisions, and making digital experiences accessible and inclusive. Communicate clearly, collaborate respectfully, ask thoughtful questions, and incorporate feedback from mentors, peers, and cross-functional partners. Explain the reasoning behind your design work, share progress, and manage scoped tasks with guidance. Be willing to explore alternatives, iterate, and learn from feedback. Exposure to user interviews or usability testing is helpful but not required. . Benefits. Eligible interns will receive a stipend to help offset summer housing and relocation costs.. Hybrid schedule, with interns generally working together in person Tuesday through Thursday..
