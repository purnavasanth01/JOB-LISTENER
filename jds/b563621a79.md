A Process Control Engineer Intern in Micron’s Technology Development team will participate in the development of process and equipment control solutions. In this position, you'll have the opportunity to collaborate with process engineering, IT, equipment automation, data engineering, and data science teams to develop and deliver high quality control strategies and sophisticated methods of variation prediction, identification, and reduction.. . Specifically, as a PCE R2R Intern, you will learn to develop specific models to identify process variation and further improve variation detection and control capabilities with an end goal of efficiency, equipment & product safety, process variation reduction, and yield improvement. You will participate in data analysis and automation activities, model building and advanced strategy development.. . Learning Opportunities May Include: . Design and implement solutions to reduce process variation and develop systems that automate or simplify process control tasks. Identify process requirements to design and deliver decision logic. Extracting data from different databases via SQL and other query languages and apply data cleansing, outlier identification, and missing data techniques.
