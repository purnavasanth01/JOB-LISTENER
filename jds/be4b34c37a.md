Note: The job is a remote job and is open to candidates in USA. The Minor Football League (MFL) is hiring a Web Design Intern to support its digital presence and user experience across web platforms. The intern will help design and update pages, create responsive interfaces and graphics, and test and improve front-end components in collaboration with marketing and operations teams.. . Responsibilities. The Web Design Intern role is a remote internship focused on supporting the league’s digital presence and user experience across its web platforms. Day-to-day tasks include assisting in designing and updating web pages, creating and refining user interfaces, and contributing to responsive layouts that work effectively across devices. The intern will collaborate with marketing and operations teams to implement graphics, visuals, and content that align with the MFL brand and engage fans, players, and partners. Responsibilities may also include testing and troubleshooting front-end components, optimizing site performance, and suggesting design improvements based on user feedback and analytics. . Skills. Candidates should possess skills in Web Design and Responsive Web Design to create visually engaging, mobile-friendly pages. Candidates should possess skills in User Interface Design to develop intuitive, accessible layouts and navigation. Candidates should possess skills in Front-End Development to implement designs using standard web technologies (e.g., HTML, CSS, basic JavaScript). Candidates should possess skills in Graphic Design to produce digital assets consistent with the MFL brand identity. Strong attention to detail, ability to follow brand and accessibility guidelines, and willingness to learn from feedback. Effective written and verbal communication skills and ability to collaborate remotely with diverse teams. Familiarity with design and prototyping tools (e.g., Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch) and basic image editing software is beneficial. Current enrollment in or recent completion of a program in Web Design, Graphic Design, Computer Science, Digital Media, or a related field is preferred. . Benefits. Remote internship. .
