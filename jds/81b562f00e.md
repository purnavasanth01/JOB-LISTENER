What's the role?. The Product Quality Engineering (PQE) team within the Quality Business Planning Group of Product & Technology is seeking a highly motivated intern with a background in Data Science, Software Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.. PQE plays a critical role in measuring, monitoring, and improving the quality, reliability, and contractual compliance of the HERE product portfolio. The team provides the engineering and analytics foundation for evaluating quality across HERE's map, location, and platform products, including customer KPI reporting for leading global automotive manufacturers.. . As part of the team's ongoing transformation, PQE is building the HERE Quality Platform (HQP), a next-generation analytics ecosystem designed to scale quality measurement and insight generation across the organization. In this role, you will contribute to the development of the Quality Analytics Hub (QAH), working alongside experienced engineers, data scientists, and product stakeholders to create scalable analytics solutions that drive product quality decisions.. . Who are you?. You will help design and implement data pipelines, analytics applications, and visualization solutions that enable data-driven quality assessments at scale. This role provides hands-on experience applying modern data science, software engineering, cloud technologies, and AI-powered capabilities to solve real-world engineering challenges.. .
