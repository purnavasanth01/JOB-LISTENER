Job Summary Software Engineer Interns will join Cambridge Technology product development teams and contribute to meaningful business and technology initiatives. Interns will gain hands-on experience across the software development lifecycle while working alongside experienced software engineers, architects, and technical leaders. Key Deliverables and Responsibilities Support product backlog priorities by designing and developing software features and components, including web programming and business-layer development. Develop code, write automated unit tests, participate in code reviews and pair programming, and contribute to build and release activities. Apply Cambridge software development lifecycle, coding, configuration, testing, and Agile team practices to assigned work. Work with relational databases, SQL, APIs, and event-driven components when applicable to the assigned project. Create test plans and test cases, perform testing, document results, and report defects as needed. Evaluate design and coding options, explain tradeoffs with guidance from experienced engineers, and communicate effectively with stakeholders and team members. What We Are Looking For Current undergraduate student pursuing Computer Science, Computer Information Systems, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Management Information Systems, Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, or a related field. Understanding of object-oriented programming, with experience in C# or a comparable programming language. Experience with .NET frameworks and ASP.NET MVC, plus an understanding of relational database systems and SQL. Interest in modern application development, automated testing, CI/CD, and Agile or Scrum practices. Problem-solving ability, initiative, accountability, eagerness to learn, and willingness to collaborate in a team environment. Exposure to tools or technologies such as Visual Studio, Azure DevOps, SQL Server Management Studio, APIs, React, Angular, Snowflake, YAML, Terraform, or Kafka is beneficial.
