## Job description (verbatim)

The Walt Disney Company’s Disney Consumer Products team is hiring a Product Design Intern to support development across accessories categories. The intern will create design and trend materials, assist with product and packaging approvals, and support creative rollouts and product sample organization.. . Responsibilities. Develop and design product mock-ups, gather images and layout creative presentations, and trend point of view boards. Collaborate with Sr. Managers and Design managers to design, approve, and review products and packaging while collaborating on feedback. Create templates for design point of view boards for creative rollouts. Pull cross-category product trends for Accessories. Assist in pulling art direction and assets for collaborations. Monitor competitors, product innovation, and market dynamics. Research Initial seasonal trends by category. Manage and organize product samples. Multi-task and prioritize multiple design projects within strict deadlines. Assist with a large volume of packaging approvals. Present our team’s deck at our creative rollouts. . Skills. Prior coursework and/or experience in Softlines categories (e.g., raw materials, silver smithing, plastic, textiles, print, pattern, graphic composition, manufacturing). Strong presentation skills with the ability to communicate ideas clearly and effectively. Demonstrated product design skills (e.g., 3D product design, technical design, sketching, creative application and manipulation of art, final mechanical art, fabric and trim identification, CAD sketches). Proficiency in: Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Keynote, Microsoft Outlook, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. Receptive to constructive feedback and able to apply direction effectively. Able to remain effective under pressure and adapt quickly to changing priorities. Pursuing a degree in Graphic Design, Fashion Design, Product Design with an emphasis on products related to Bags, Backpacks, Footwear, Headwear, Hosiery, Jewelry, Timing, and Vision. A portfolio review is required for this position. Be enrolled in an accredited college/university, pursuing a degree, taking at least one class at the time of application, OR currently participating in a Disney College Program or Disney Internship. Have not completed one year of continual employment on a Disney Internship or Disney College Program. Be at least 18 years of age. Possess unrestricted work authorization. Prior experience in jewelry design. Coursework and/or experience in silversmithing. Skilled in creating mood boards/trend boards. Senior year preferred. . Benefits.
