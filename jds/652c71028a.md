Union Home Mortgage Corp. provides a real-world internship program designed to help students learn about the industry, develop skills, and gain professional experience. The UI/UX Designer Intern collaborates with product, engineering, business, and user stakeholders to transform workflows and user needs into intuitive digital experiences, using modern and AI-assisted prototyping tools.. . Responsibilities. Work with Product Owners, engineers, business partners, and users to understand business problems, workflows, and user needs. Translate business processes, user needs, and product ideas into intuitive user flows and digital experiences. · Create user flows, interaction models, screen designs, and interactive prototypes. · Use modern design and AI-assisted prototyping tools to rapidly move from an idea to an experience users can see and interact with. · Create functional prototypes that allow stakeholders and users to meaningfully experience and evaluate potential solutions. Look for opportunities to remove unnecessary steps, simplify decisions, reduce cognitive load, and improve usability. Challenge existing user experiences rather than simply reproducing existing screens or workflows. Observe how users perform their work and incorporate those observations into design decisions. Explore multiple approaches to solving user problems and rapidly iterate based on feedback. Collaborate with Product Owners to visualize potential solutions during process discovery and early product development. Partner with AI Software Engineers to help translate validated experiences into production applications. Learn and apply reusable design patterns and contribute to the organization's design system. Consider accessibility, responsive behavior, consistency, and usability throughout the design process. Present and demonstrate prototypes to users, stakeholders, and team members. . Skills. To perform this role successfully, an individual should demonstrate creativity, empathy for users, problem-solving ability, and an interest in using emerging technologies to create better digital experiences. Strong interest in user experience, interaction design, product design, and technology. Ability to think about complex processes from the perspective of the person performing them. Curiosity about user behavior and an instinct for simplifying complex interactions. Ability to think beyond existing screens and workflows to imagine simpler ways for users to accomplish an outcome. Strong visual and conceptual communication skills. Interest in creating interactive and functional prototypes rather than only static visual designs. Familiarity with modern UI/UX design or prototyping tools. Interest in AI-assisted design and prototyping tools. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Ability to work through ambiguous concepts and rapidly explore potential solutions. Self-motivated with strong written and verbal communication skills. Willingness to work both independently and collaboratively with business and technical partners. Pursuing a 4-year college degree in User Experience Design, Interaction Design, Product Design, Human-Computer Interaction, Visual Communication Design, Information Systems, Information Technology, or a related field. Familiarity with design and prototyping tools such as Figma or similar platforms. Coursework, personal projects, portfolios, or other examples demonstrating an interest in user experience and problem-solving are preferred.
