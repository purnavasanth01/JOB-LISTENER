Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all . . Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.. . The Segment Marketing team at Micron is responsible for understanding the markets and customers we serve and shaping how Micron’s memory and storage solutions win in the mobile and client computing space. We partner across product management, sales, engineering, and business units to deliver market insights, compelling positioning, and go-to-market strategies that drive growth with key OEM customers.. . Position Overview . As a Segment Marketing Intern, you will contribute to Micron’s market strategy and customer engagement. This role blends market research, competitive analysis, and strategic marketing, offering exposure to cross-functional collaboration, key customer accounts, and groundbreaking technologies.. . Responsibilities . Support the Segment Marketing team in developing market strategy for Micron’s Client and Mobile business.. Analyze market trends, segment size (TAM), and demand drivers.. Conduct competitive analysis of products, pricing, and positioning across key competitors.. Gather and synthesize customer and OEM insights to identify needs, opportunities, and market shifts.. Help develop product positioning, messaging, and value propositions.. Create customer engagement materials, sales enablement content, and product presentations.. Present findings and recommendations to marketing leadership while identifying risks and opportunities to strengthen MCBU’s market position.. Leverage AI tools for market research, data analysis, and presentation generation.. . Minimum Qualifications . Current Bachelor’s (Senior) or Master’s student in Marketing, Business Administration, Business Analytics, Electrical or Computer Engineering, or equivalent experience. Must be graduating no sooner than September 2027.. Strong analytical skills with experience in market research and data analysis.. Ability to prioritize tasks efficiently and work diligently in a fast-paced environment.. Excellent presentation and communication abilities.. Comfortable using AI tools to improve workflow efficiency and insight generation.. . Preferred Qualifications . Ability to translate technical product information into clear customer-facing messaging.. Knowledge of market research sources, SharePoint, or dashboarding platforms.. Previous experience in marketing, market research, or business strategy, ideally in a technical field. . . . . .
