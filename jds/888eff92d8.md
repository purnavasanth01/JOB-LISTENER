## Job description (verbatim)

Roku is hiring an AI Engineering Software Engineer Intern to build and evaluate AI agents, copilots, and automated workflows for its products and platforms. The intern will deliver a scoped engineering project, connect agents to data and tools, and collaborate with engineers and mentors.. . Responsibilities. Own a well-defined project that contributes to the success of your team, from defining the problem through to delivery. Build and test AI agents, copilots and automated workflows that perform meaningful, multi-step work. Connect agents to trusted data, services and internal tools, using approaches such as retrieval, tool calling and orchestration, and help define what an agent may do on its own and when it should hand over to a person. Evaluate your work: measure whether it does the job well, check its reliability and failure modes, and keep improving it based on real feedback. Use AI coding tools every day to move faster, and validate everything they produce through testing, review and your own judgment. Write clean, well-tested, documented code, take part in code reviews, and collaborate with engineers, mentors and cross-functional partners. . Skills. You are a penultimate-year student, or a final-year student planning to go on to a one-year master's course. You hold a permanent right to work in the UK. You are keen to work with AI technologies and have previous experience building AI agents and automated workflows. Currently enrolled in a degree program in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, Engineering or a related field. Proficiency in Python, ideally with projects, open source or hackathon work beyond your coursework. Hands-on experience building an LLM-powered or agentic system that does multi-step work, and ideally that someone other than you has used. Familiarity with agentic patterns such as grounding and retrieval, tool use, orchestration, memory or conversational interfaces. Sound engineering judgment: you check AI-generated work, understand how it can fail, and recognise when simple automation or a human review is the better approach. A solid grounding in computer science fundamentals such as data structures, algorithms, and operating systems or networking concepts. Strong problem-solving and technical communication abilities, and the ability to break down ambiguous problems and deliver in small steps. . Benefits.
