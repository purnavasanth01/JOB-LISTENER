## Job description (verbatim)

Teradata is hiring a Software Engineer Intern for its Agentic Database engineering team. The intern will build and measure a production-minded database component that enables AI agents to store, retrieve, and reason over enterprise data, including implementation, benchmarking, testing, and integration with platform services.. . Responsibilities. Design and implement a scoped feature in the Agentic Database, such as a Postgres extension, a query-path optimization, or an agent-facing service, taking it from a written design through code review to merge. Build a benchmark and test harness that replays representative agent workloads (many small, concurrent, bursty reads and writes) and tracks latency, throughput, and resource cost. Analyze query plans, storage behavior, and concurrency to find bottlenecks, then prototype fixes and report measured before-and-after results. Write unit, integration, and regression tests that keep your component correct as the engine and its extensions evolve. Integrate your component with adjacent platform services such as Teradata Fabric, Context Engine, Enterprise MCP, and AgentStack, and validate that agents can reach data through governed interfaces. Document design decisions, trade-offs, and results in written notes that engineers outside the team can follow and act on. Present your progress in team design reviews and your final results in an end-of-program demo to engineering leaders and product partners. . Skills. Currently enrolled in a BS, MS, or PhD program in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field. Proficiency in at least one systems or backend language, such as C, C++, Rust, Go, or Python, demonstrated through coursework or projects. Coursework or project experience in databases, operating systems, or distributed systems (for example indexing, transactions, query processing, or concurrency). Working knowledge of SQL and relational database concepts. Hands-on experience with PostgreSQL, such as writing extensions, reading internals, or tuning performance. Exposure to LLM agents or tool-calling frameworks, including the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Open-source contributions or a personal project involving databases, storage engines, or systems performance. . Benefits. A dedicated engineering mentor, regular 1:1s with your manager, and design and code reviews with senior and staff engineers throughout the program.. End-to-end ownership of a scoped project, from design and implementation to benchmarking and a written summary of findings the team can build on.. Practical skill in benchmarking and performance analysis for agent workloads.. A view into how Teradata’s agentic platform fits together across Fabric, Enterprise MCP, and AgentStack, plus a chance to present your work to engineering leadership at the end of the program..
