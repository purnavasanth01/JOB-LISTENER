Note: The job is a remote job and is open to candidates in USA. The Foundation for California Community Colleges is recruiting C2C Forward Fellows to work within the Office of Cradle-to-Career Data. The Data Programs Fellow will develop data tools and analytical reports, support research and policy analysis, and contribute to the growth of California’s statewide data system.. . Responsibilities. Fellows on data programs will develop dashboards, query builder tools, and analytical data reports to inform research, advocacy efforts, and policy analysis. Fellows will be responsible for advancing the work outlined in Cradle-to-Career’s annual work plan. . Skills. Qualified candidates have a minimum of 5 years of experience in one or more of the following criteria:. Demonstrated portfolio that you have implemented analytical and/or digital tools such as dashboards, user-center design, research request tools, secure data enclaves, modern data stacks, and more. Experience Staff employed in higher education labs focusing on education, economics, public policy, or relevant areas of research. Curriculum development for interacting and engaging with public facing data tools. Computer science background and/or ability to code in one or more languages. Experience building and/or maintaining large databases. Quantitative research and analysis. PhD in applied research. Master of Public Policy alumni. High profile innovative projects. Experience with tokenization of sensitive data elements. Must be able to accommodate a January 2027 start date. Extensive knowledge of quantitative and qualitative research methodologies. Knowledge of workforce and higher education admissions, policies, and legislation. Direct experience or knowledge of current challenges in data systems, college applications, and transfer processes. Experience in providing direct support services to students or experience as an administrator in an educational setting. Experience conveying quantitative and qualitative research findings in many formats. Strong oral and written communication skills. Project management experience and ability to stay highly organized. Ability to work independently and collaboratively in moderate direction. Statistical competency in at least one data analytic programming language such as Python, R, Stata, and SQL. Experience with data visualization tools such as Tableau. Experience working with large administrative data sets. Experience collaborating with both technical and non-technical teams. Extensive knowledge of Learning and Employment Records. Understanding of Open Science best practices. Experience cleaning and merging complex and incomplete datasets. Experience working with government programs, policies, operations, and/or data. Has professional or educational experience in education, workforce development, policy, economics, or similar social science field or within government. Excellent project management and organizational skills. Ability to communicate statistical results to diverse audiences, both in text and in graphical form. Exceptional communication and interpersonal skills. An interest in designing and participating in project processes that incorporate both quantitative and qualitative research methods and practices. Curiosity and willingness to learn about state agencies and the role they play. Flexibility, self-motivation, and the ability to manage multiple tasks efficiently in a team. The ability to continually track large numbers of projects and tasks and to juggle multiple competing priorities in a fast-paced environment. We're recruiting for mission-driven, passionate, equity-minded individuals. Identify as a subject matter expert in one of the following categories:. Early childhood education. K-12 Education. Higher Education. Labor Economist/ Workforce Development. Health and Social Services. Computer science, data infrastructure, software engineering, cloud computing. You are a systems and platform thinker who loves enabling data teams with tools to do their work efficiently and effectively.
