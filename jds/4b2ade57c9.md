Federal Express Corporation is hiring a Summer Intern to support Customer Experience initiatives that improve transit time data accuracy across the Caribbean and Central America. The intern will analyze operational and service data, help validate and update transit time models, and communicate findings and recommendations to stakeholders.. . Responsibilities. Support the review, validation, and cleanup of transit time data across the Caribbean and Central America region. Analyze transit time information across multiple service offerings, including priority freight and deferred freight, to identify outdated assumptions, inconsistencies, and opportunities for correction. Review historical transit time adjustments and support the validation of service standards against current operational conditions. Assist in evaluating and updating legacy transit time models used to support system-wide service and operational decisions. Analyze holiday schedules and other service-related variables that may impact transit time calculations and customer commitments. Compare service performance by country before and after data corrections to evaluate the impact of changes and identify additional improvement opportunities. Leverage existing dashboards and Power BI tools to analyze service levels, monitor key performance indicators, and communicate findings. Support technical modeling and data analysis to improve the accuracy and reliability of transit time information. Translate analytical findings into clear insights and recommendations that support service quality and the customer value proposition. Collaborate with Customer Experience, engineering, and cross-functional teams to understand business requirements and support data-driven decision-making. Present project progress, analytical findings, and recommendations to management and key stakeholders. Participate in various networking and/or community service events.
