As an Offensive Security Intern, you will support the team’s efforts to proactively identify and reduce cyber risk through attack surface management, vulnerability research, security assessments, and automation initiatives. You will gain hands-on experience supporting Continuous Threat Exposure Management (CTEM), external attack surface monitoring, and security validation activities while contributing to initiatives that strengthen JM Family’s overall security posture. Responsibilities: Support attack surface management and vulnerability analysis by identifying, researching, validating, and documenting externally exposed assets, vulnerabilities, and security findings. Assist with penetration testing, security research, threat modeling, attack simulations, and security testing of applications, cloud environments, and infrastructure under the guidance of senior team members. Develop scripts, dashboards, and process improvements that enhance vulnerability management, Continuous Threat Exposure Management (CTEM), and executive reporting capabilities. Support external attack surface monitoring and security validation activities to improve visibility into potential threats and provide actionable security insights. Help improve the efficiency of offensive security processes through security automation and enhancements to recurring analysis and reporting. Contribute to cybersecurity initiatives that support the protection of customer and business information, reduction of enterprise risk, secure innovation, and continuous improvement.
