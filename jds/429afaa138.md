## Job description (verbatim)

As the Software Engineering Resident, you are an AI-Native Engineer on Method's internal product team. The people who will use the product are your customers. You sit with them, understand the problem, and build the answer in front of them, working with AI agents that write much of the code while you own what ships.. You will work alongside the Product Management and Design Residents from the first conversation, not at the end of a hand-off. Together you shape the problem, write the specifications, and turn them into a working, shippable product.. This residency is practical. The tools will change between now and when you start, and again before you finish. The residency teaches what does not change: framing a problem, directing AI to solve it, knowing when the output is wrong, and being able to explain every line you ship.. Today, our internal product is built with React, TypeScript and REST APIs , using AI coding agents. Expect that to have moved on by the time you join.. The Engineering Resident role comprises three primary learning areas:. Build with agents: Break problems into specifications an AI agent can act on, direct the agent, and review, test and take ownership of what comes back.. Build in front of the customer: Prototype quickly with the people who have the problem, show working software early, and turn their reaction into the next iteration.. Keep the work trustworthy: Learn what may and may not be shared with which AI tool, keep a clear record of what the AI produced and who reviewed it, and never ship code you cannot explain.. Key Responsibilities (Learning & Contribution): . Discovery: Sit with the people who use the internal product, ask questions, and help define the problem before anyone writes code.. Rapid Prototyping: Use AI tools to put working prototypes in front of users in days, not weeks.. Specifications: Co-write user stories and acceptance criteria with the Product and Design Residents, written so that both a teammate and an AI agent can act on them.. Agent-Driven Development: Build features across the product (interface, APIs and data) by directing AI coding agents, and use those agents to find your way around the codebase quickly.. Judging AI Output: Review everything an agent produces. Test it, question it, and catch it when it is wrong.. Quality: Write and maintain automated tests, and use them as the check that agent-produced work does what the specification says.. Data & Privacy: Learn Method's rules on what data may go to which AI tool, and follow them without supervision by the end of the residency.. Provenance & Review: Keep a clear trail of what the AI produced, who reviewed it and what changed. Take part in code reviews with senior engineers (mentors), giving and receiving direct feedback.. Understanding What You Ship: Be able to explain how every part of your work functions. AI speeds up the building; it does not replace the understanding.. Cost Awareness: Notice what agent-driven work costs to run, and factor that into how you work.. Communication: Explain progress, trade-offs and technical constraints plainly to non-technical teammates and to the customer.. Sharing What You Learn: Bring what you discover about working with AI back to the wider Resident cohort.. Qualifications: . You have built something that works (coursework, a bootcamp project, a side project) and can talk through how you built it.. You have used AI tools (coding assistants, agents, chat models) to build software, and can describe a time the AI got it wrong and how you caught it.. Foundational understanding of core web technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) and at least one modern front-end framework. React is preferred.. Familiarity with version control (Git) and GitHub or GitLab.. Comfortable working with people who are not engineers: asking questions, explaining what you have built, and hearing that it is not what they wanted.. Strong problem-solving skills and a proactive attitude toward debugging and fixing issues.. No professional experience required.
