ABOUT STRAVA Strava is the app for active people. With over 200 million athletes in more than 185 countries, it’s more than tracking workouts—it’s where people make progress together, from new habits to new personal bests. No matter your sport or how you track it, Strava’s got you covered. Find your crew, crush your goals, and make every effort count. Start your journey https://www.strava.com/subscription with Strava today. Our mission is simple: to motivate people to live their best active lives. We believe in the power of movement to connect and drive people forward. As an Engineering Intern at Strava, you will be embedded in a team working on real projects that ship to our athletes and within the company, treated like a full-time member of the engineering team. You'll collaborate with cross functional teams that help us support and deliver to our athletes faster, while keeping them inspired and motivated. We’ll give you experienced mentors and all the tools you need to make a meaningful impact on our product. To see examples of what past interns have contributed, read our Engineering Blog https://medium.com/strava-engineering/tagged/internships! AVAILABLE INTERNSHIP: Strava is looking for software engineering interns for a range of disciplines. The Comms and Experimentation Platform team is part of the Horizontal Engineering organization. We build the infrastructure behind how Strava connects with athletes. Our systems deliver personalized and timely communications to athletes at scale, so reliability and performance are critical to our success. Our work supports marketing and product development teams. We follow a flexible hybrid model that requires you in our SF office four days per week. WHAT YOU’LL DO: - Build and ship a well-scoped project on our communications or experimentation platform with support from senior engineers - Write clean, well-tested code and get feedback through code review and mentorship - Learn how event-driven systems and data pipelines work at scale - Collaborate with teammates and cross-functional partners in Marketing and Product to understand what they need from the platform - Contribute ideas in team discussions, planning, and demos WE’RE EXCITED ABOUT YOU BECAUSE: - You have programming experience in at least one language (Scala, Java, Python, Go, or similar) through coursework, projects, or prior work. Full-stack experience (React, Typescript) is a plus - You understand core fundamentals like data structures, APIs, and basic databases - You’re eager to learn, ask great questions, and aren’t afraid to work on unfamiliar problems - You work well with others, communicate clearly, and actively seek feedback - You are currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, with an expected graduation date of May 2027, December 2027, or May 2028, OR have recently completed or are currently enrolled in a coding bootcamp, technical training program, or are transitioning into software engineering through equivalent practical experience. THE SUMMER INTERNSHIP PROGRAM: - The program will be in our San Francisco, CA office with a hybrid office attendance requirement of 4 days per week - Housing Stipend for Summer is provided to live in SF for 3 months - Length of program: 10-12 weeks (Summer 2027 roughly June through August). - Working Hours: 40 hours per week - This is a paid internship We will be closing applications for this role on Fri, Nov 27 at 5:00pm PST.
