Applied Materials’ High Current Product Engineering department is seeking a co-op to join our team in 2027 Spring! This role focuses on applying AI to two areas: large-scale data analysis and multivariable process tuning. . . Key Responsibilities: . Analyze large process, metrology, and equipment datasets using AI and machine learning tools to identify trends, anomalies, and improvement opportunities. Apply AI-driven optimization techniques (e.g., Bayesian optimization, design of experiments, and surrogate modeling) to tune multiple process and equipment parameters at the same time, speeding up recipe development and improving process performance. Lead small development projects that apply AI models to wafer metrology and test data, and present findings and recommendations to the engineering team. Build, test, and document AI-assisted analysis workflows and scripts to automate recurring data tasks. . Start Date: January 2027 for 6+ months ONSITE at our Gloucester, MA location .
