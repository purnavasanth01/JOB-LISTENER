Job purpose We are looking for a student to assist in the verification of embedded software for transfer cases. The student will be part of a Software Validation Team and work together with other groups to support activities such as test execution, work product reviews, troubleshooting, data acquisition, documentation and scripting. There may be opportunities to experience the impact of our software and controls across the test bench, dynamometer, virtual simulation, and vehicle environments. The student will learn about software testing best practices and gain a broad perspective related to planning, teamwork, requirements, electronics, development process, and various tools. Key responsibilities Configure tools and write scripts to assist in software verification Develop and execute test cases to verify software Generate test reports Automate various processes and tasks Create documentation for processes and procedures Build & maintain test bench environments Collect and analyse data Troubleshoot test environments What we’re looking for Student interested in verification of embedded software Basic understanding of electrical circuits Experience with coding or scripting Strong writing and communication skills What we believe Inclusion-We value diversity in people, ideas, and experiences.
