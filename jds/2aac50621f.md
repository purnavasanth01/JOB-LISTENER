Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all . . Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.. . Welcome to an outstanding opportunity at Micron, where we advance technology, products, and innovation! As an Intern in our Technology-AMAIS group, you will work directly on semiconductor process or structure modeling projects. You will help validate and deploy advanced modeling AI solutions that support new technology acceleration.. . Responsibilities:. Identify relationships between process conditions, equipment parameters, metrology, electrical performance, and yield.. Assist in modeling semiconductor processes such as lithography, plasma etch, thin-film deposition, CMP, diffusion, and thermal processes.. Apply statistical analysis, DOE, physics-based modeling, and machine learning to identify critical sources of process variation.. Assist with the development and validation of virtual process or structure models, composite, and predictive models.. Analyze wafer-level spatial variation and manufacturing datasets using tools like Python or R or equivalent experience.. Develop models linking process, structure, electrical behavior, and yield.. Translate analysis into clear engineering recommendations and present results to Technology-AMAIS teams.. . Minimum Qualifications:. Currently pursuing an M.S. or Ph.D. or equivalent experience in Electrical Engineering, Materials Science, Chemical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Applied Physics, or a related technical field.. Coursework or research experience in semiconductor processing, materials, or process integration.. Experience with semiconductor processes including lithography, etch, deposition, CMP, diffusion, implantation, contacts, or interconnects.. Understanding of basic statistics, regression, DOE, ML, DL, and AI techniques. Ability to communicate technical findings clearly and work collaboratively with multidisciplinary engineering teams.. . Preferred Qualifications:. Graduate research involving modeling of semiconductor fabrication, process integration, device physics, computational materials/physics, or advanced packaging.. Familiarity with DRAM, NAND, CMOS, HBM, or semiconductor manufacturing.. Experience with developing or using simulation platforms such as Silvaco, SPX, SEM3D, COMSOL Multiphysics, Ansys, AI platforms.. Experience with AI modeling for metrology such as SEM/TEM, CD-SEM, overlay, defect inspection, electrical characterization, or wafer-level measurements.. Programming and data-science experience using Python, pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, or similar libraries.. Exposure to machine learning, Gaussian processes, Bayesian optimization, surrogate modeling, or reduced-order modeling.. Interest in AI-enabled semiconductor process development and virtual process modeling.. .
