## Job description (verbatim)

PACCAR is hiring a Vehicle Software Integration Engineer Intern for its Innovation team. The intern will integrate a next-generation connected vehicle platform into a Class 8 truck, including installation, validation, and debugging across hardware, software, and vehicle networks.. . Responsibilities. Studying the truck's electrical, electronic and software systems and overall vehicle architecture to understand how the new platform must fit into it. Identifying the hardware and software changes needed for integration, then putting them in place. Installing the platform in the truck, bringing it up, and validating it. Debugging issues across hardware, software, and vehicle networks. Identifying technical risks early and proposing smart ways to mitigate them early in the program. Coming up with creative solutions when standard approaches don't work. Documenting your work and demonstrating the results to engineers and leadership. . Skills. Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Mechatronics, Automotive Engineering, or a related program. Strong understanding of vehicle systems and how they work together. Strong understanding of vehicle networking protocols, such as CAN, automotive Ethernet and service-oriented communication (SOME/IP, DDS) or similar. Programming and debugging in C/C++, Java or Kotlin; scripting in Python or shell. Comfort with Linux: command line, Git, building and cross-compiling software. Foundational understanding of vehicle electrical power and grounding, and the ability to read schematics and wiring diagrams. Hands-on project experience bringing hardware and software together. Operating-system fundamentals. Hands-on experience with CAN, SAE J1939, DBC files, SocketCAN and tools such as CANoe, Kvaser or PCAN. Hands-on experience with automotive Ethernet, Ethernet switches and VLANs, NAT/firewall, Wireshark or tcpdump. Experience with real-time operating systems and platforms such as QNX or AUTOSAR, and with hypervisors (QNX Hypervisor, Xen or similar). Android or Android Automotive development (Java or Kotlin), ADB/fastboot, Vehicle HAL. Embedded Linux and Yocto, bootloaders or device trees. Hands-on experience with automotive development platforms, such as those from Qualcomm, NXP or Infineon, or other Arm-based platforms. Linux-based telematics (TCU) platforms, connectivity SDKs such as Qualcomm TelSDK, AT commands or GNSS. Display, audio or camera integration. Truck or automotive electrical systems: 12/24 V power, ignition, harnesses and connectors. Working-level understanding of OTA software updates or containers. .
