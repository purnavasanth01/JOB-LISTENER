Develop an interactive Shiny app that predicts placebo response rates and variability for future Phase 2/3 AD clinical trials based on population and design characteristics, enabling proactive trial design optimization. This internship focuses on developing an interactive benchmarking platform to predict placebo responses in Atopic Dermatitis (AD) clinical trials based on planned study population characteristics. The intern will leverage the AD Placebo iDB (internal database) to build a predictive modeling framework and deploy it as a user-friendly Shiny application. This project focuses on AD clinical trials, where Placebo response is variable and influenced by multiple factors. The AD Placebo iDB (internal database) contains rich historical data across diverse populations and study designs. Accurate placebo prediction is critical for sample size planning, and go/no-go decisions. An interactive tool would improve efficiency and facilitate access to historical insights across clinical and statistical teams. After exploring the data, the intern will develop a predictive modeling for placebo response.
