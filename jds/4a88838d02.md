## Job description (verbatim)

Working alongside other data scientists, engineers, and product managers, you'll turn product questions into analyses, build models and metrics, and share insights that shape what CoreAI builds next. Take ownership of product and business questions, break them down into clear analyses, and choose the right data, methods, and algorithms to answer them. Build an understanding of CoreAI products and the customers who use them, and use that context to make your analyses relevant and actionable. Explore large datasets, such as product usage data, to find trends, surface new questions and opportunities, and understand what the data can and can't tell us. Design and analyze experiments, such as A/B tests, to measure the impact of product changes and help teams decide what to ship. Analyze and validate results, and build metrics, dashboards, and automated reports or data pipelines to track outcomes over time. Partner with engineers, product managers, and other data scientists to share clear, compelling insights that help improve products and services used by millions of customers.
