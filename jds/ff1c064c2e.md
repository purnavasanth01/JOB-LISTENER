What You’ll Do . Our ML Compiler team builds the software that runs PyTorch models on Samsung’s compute-near-memory AI accelerator: a PyTorch device and compiler backend, a Triton-based kernel compiler and an LLVM back end. We work closely with the runtime and hardware teams and with the PyTorch and Triton open-source communities. Over the summer you’ll own one piece of making ordinary PyTorch models fast on new memory-centric hardware, from research idea to merged, measured code. . Location: Daily onsite presence at our San Jose Headquarters in alignment with our Flexible Work policy . Reports to: Director . Design a search space for Triton kernel configurations on the accelerator: tile sizes, launch-grid mapping, on-chip buffer budgets and load style. . Build legality rules with our compiler engineers that prune configurations that will not compile or will not fit in on-chip memory. . Develop a kernel cost model, calibrated against our simulator and profiler, that ranks candidates so only the most promising are benchmarked. . Build a reliable benchmarking harness and a persistent tuning cache, so that tuning cost stays practical. . Integrate the tuner with the torch.compile Inductor autotuning path and with Triton’s autotuner. . Extend tuning to data placement: tune kernels for a given tensor layout and export tuned costs that a placement pass can use. . Measure kernel-level and end-to-end LLM inference gains, and share results in weekly syncs and a final presentation. . Collaborate with PyTorch and Triton developers on upstream autotuning hooks, and hand off documented, tested code.. . Complete other responsibilities as assigned. . What You Bring .
