## Job description (verbatim)

To make electronics, you need chips, wafers, transistors, reticles, and... To make these, you must see, test and manufacture them at scale—faster and better than ever before. That's where KLA comes in. Whether you're early in your career or an experienced professional, you'll solve complex challenges, work alongside brilliant minds and help shape the future of technology.. . . Group/Division . The Semiconductor Products and Customers (Semi PC) business unit designs, builds and sells KLA’s product portfolio to help chip manufacturers meet high-quality standards for technologies like AI, data centers, automotive and electronic devices. Our inspection, metrology, and analytics systems detect and monitor defects across logic and memory chips, specialty semiconductors, wafers, reticles, advanced packaging, process equipment and materials. We also develop technologies for specialty processes, IC substrates, and PCB manufacturing, including etch and deposition, component inspection, and imaging and analytics. The Film and Scatterometry Technology (FaST) division delivers industry-leading metrology solutions essential to advanced semiconductor manufacturing. Its portfolio includes systems for measuring film thickness, critical dimensions, composition, and resistivity—key to developing and scaling next-gen IC processes. FaST partners closely with customers from early R&D through high-volume production, ensuring performance and process alignment. . . What You'll Do . In this role, you will play a key part in advancing business priorities by delivering high-impact work across your area of expertise.. . Key responsibilities and skills: . Own NPI deliverables, related to product BOMs, procedures, tooling, fixtures.. Lead multi-functional team (project manager) to meet key milestones, while meeting project requirements.. Key participant in the design and development process; Propose new ideas and improvements, determine and review requirements, and drives crucial project decisions. Experiencing, contributing to, and owning components in the critical PLC process.. Coordinate prototype material, build, align, and test opto-mechanical, mechanical, and electrical assemblies for design validation and assembly procedure development.. Generate and release the product Bill of Material (BOM) and work with technical writers to provide valuable input to generate assembly, integration, and test procedures.. Drive the development of the alignment/test tooling and packaging that are required to build and ship assemblies from our vendors to our manufacturing facility and the field.. Troubleshoot and resolve issues with new designs and prototypes through assembly, testing, and system integration in a cleanroom environment.. Possess strong communication skills and make decisions based on data..
