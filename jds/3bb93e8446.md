## Job description (verbatim)

InsCipher is seeking a part-time AI Product Intern for the BYU Winter 2027 semester. The intern will contribute to product enhancements, including API improvements, UX/UI upgrades, and AI-powered prototypes, working with product managers, engineers, and users.. . Responsibilities. Own assigned projects from start to finish and deliver on agreed deadlines. Research user and client needs and turn them into clear problem statements and requirements, and bring forward ideas to improve legacy products and shape new ones, backed by research and a clear case for the user and the business. Write user stories, acceptance criteria, and PRDs in Jira and Confluence, and help groom backlogs, track sprints, and keep documentation current. Design and test UX and UI improvements, from user journey maps and early sketches to polished mockups the design and engineering teams can build from. Build fast prototypes and proofs of concept using AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and AI coding assistants, and test them with real users. Review and test APIs and integrations, comparing real responses against the documentation, and document gaps and requirements for improvements. Help troubleshoot client issues and trace them to their root cause, and join client calls as needed for support and feedback. Work closely with product managers and our onshore and offshore engineering teams in Lithuania and Bulgaria, joining standups, sprint planning, and demos. Report progress, blockers, and results to the product team on a regular cadence. Required to perform other duties as requested, directed, or assigned. . Skills. Currently enrolled at BYU and majoring in a science degree; desired majors include Machine Learning, Data Science, ACME (Applied and Computational Mathematics), Computer Science, Information Systems, Business Strategy, and Math. Strong aptitude for problem solving; candidates will complete an aptitude assessment as part of the hiring process, and we are looking for top scorers. Heavy, hands-on experience with AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Cursor, or similar, with examples of how they have been used to build, analyze, or create. Business-minded and user-focused: asks why a feature matters and who it helps, notices when something is confusing, and has a good design eye for layout, clarity, and visual polish. Dependable and self-directed: keeps a consistent schedule, meets deadlines, follows through without reminders, owns the outcome, and stays organized and comfortable with ambiguity while juggling several threads. Clear written and verbal communication, including with teammates whose first language is not English. Familiarity with REST APIs, JSON, or tools like Postman, some coding experience (Python, JavaScript, SQL, or similar), or experience with Figma or another design tool preferred. Exposure to Jira, Confluence, or agile and scrum practices, or a portfolio, GitHub, or side project that shows what has been built, preferred. Interest in insurance, fintech, or regulated industries preferred (no prior insurance knowledge needed). . Benefits. A dynamic, inclusive work environment where your ideas are valued, and your contributions lead to real change.. Work on cutting-edge projects that are reshaping an industry. Collaborate with a team of passionate, like-minded professionals. A culture that values flexibility, innovation, and personal growth. . Company Overview. InsCipher provides tax filing and insurance automation solutions services. It is a sub-organization of Veracity Insurance Solutions. It was founded in 2016, and is headquartered in Pleasant Grove, Utah, USA, with a workforce of 51-200 employees. Its website is https://www.inscipher.com.. .
