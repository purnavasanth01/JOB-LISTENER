## Job description (verbatim)

Spira AI builds autonomous AI influencers for social media. The AI Agent Product Manager intern will shape user workflows for briefing, monitoring, intervening in, and coordinating AI agents, using research, prototypes, product specifications, and collaboration with engineers and designers to ship features.. . Responsibilities. Design workflows for briefing agents, tracking progress, intervening, and reviewing output. Make responsibilities and handoffs clear when multiple agents work together. Interview creators, marketers, and small businesses, run usability tests, and turn what you learn into product requirements. Write short PRDs, user stories, and acceptance criteria with engineers. Work through edge cases such as failed runs, retries, long-running jobs, and ambiguous instructions. Stay involved through QA and launch. Prototype workflows with prompts, no-code tools, or AI coding tools and test them with users. Define what good agent output looks like, write test cases, and review results with engineering. Use activation, drop-off, and retention data to find friction in agent workflows. Track how other agent and creator tools solve similar problems. . Skills. Regular hands-on use of AI agents or LLM tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Cursor. You understand how prompts, context, tools, and memory affect their behavior and where they can fail. Curiosity about customer needs and the ability to turn research into clear, actionable specs. Comfort reading a funnel, analyzing data in a spreadsheet or SQL, and explaining what you find. Clear writing, sound technical questions, and the ability to weigh trade-offs with engineers and designers. Ownership and follow-through, including flagging blockers early. A technical background or a side project built with LLMs, agent frameworks, or workflow tools. Experience with product, design, engineering, research, or consulting, including user interviews, usability testing, or Figma wireframes.
