## Job description (verbatim)

BimBuildings/ScanPlan develops solutions that convert interior building scans into Building Information Models. The intern will work with data scientists to turn AI model outputs, LiDAR point clouds, and meshes into parametric 3D models, including reconstructed polyhedrons and Breps.. . Responsibilities. You will work with our data scientists to convert the output of AI models to parametric 3D models. You use mesh and point clouds and reconstruct polyhedrons and Breps. You’re eager to document the progress to share advancements in your work to colleagues and other interested parties. . Skills. Good understanding in geometry modeling and numerical optimization techniques is necessary. You have graduated or are at the final stage of your studies. Bachelor's or master's degree in computer graphics, computer science, civil engineering, geoscience or a related field. Experience in Python and C++. Experience with computational geometry libraries: CGAL, Grasshopper3D, VTK, Pyvista. Familiar with lidar and mesh processing tools and algorithms: open3d, pcl, meshlab. Familiar with Autodesk Revit, Dynamo, Rhino3D, Unity as well as Linux OS. You are a EU citizen or have a work permit in The Netherlands. The ideal candidate has a background in computational geometry, computer graphics, computer vision and lidar, as well as a passion for learning and exploring new technologies. Background in image processing, computer vision and mixed reality is a plus. You enjoy working together in a dynamic team in which we challenge each other to improve. Open to innovation and rethinking solutions. Table tennis or darts experience is a plus. . Benefits. Learning from each other, including training and courses, and learning on the job. A pleasant, well-equipped workspace with coffee or tea. A great working atmosphere and supportive colleagues. Breakfast and lunch at the office included. Hybrid work (remote and Zwolle office). . Company Overview. BimBuildings B.V. provides BIM modelling, 3D scanning, AI software and training services for construction projects. It was founded in 2016, and is headquartered in Zwolle, Overijssel, NLD, with a workforce of 11-50 employees. Its website is https://bimbuildings.nl.. .
