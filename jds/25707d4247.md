## Job description (verbatim)

Join Method as part of our 2027 Summer Residency Program as a Product Management Resident and help build, ship, and learn as part of a small cross-functional team of Product, Design, and Engineering Residents.. Method is a strategic design and product development consultancy with 25 years of experience, and a GlobalLogic company within the Hitachi family. This residency is a hands-on way to see how a global digital consultancy works from the inside: you will discover user needs, define solutions, and drive development alongside the people who do this for clients every day.. Product management is changing quickly as AI takes on more of the research, synthesis, specification, and reporting work. We built this residency for people who want to learn the craft as it is being rewritten, not as it was practiced five years ago.. The role: Product Management for the AI era . The modern Product Manager is less a document producer and more a decision-maker who directs, checks and refines work done with AI. At Method we describe this as AI-native product management: you still own the "why" and the "what," but you use AI to move from research to a testable idea in days rather than weeks, and you apply judgment to what it produces.. As a Resident, you will practice the skills that set an AI-native PM apart:. Framing the problem well. Writing clear specs and giving AI the right context, so the output is useful the first time.. Working with AI as a teammate. Using AI for research synthesis, prototyping and drafting, and knowing which tasks to delegate and which to keep.. Verifying, not trusting. Checking AI-generated work for accuracy, bias and fit with real user needs before it reaches a decision.. Knowing where AI belongs. Judging where AI creates value for a user or business, and saying plainly where it will not pay off.. Leading people through change. Helping teams adopt new tools and ways of working so they actually stick.. Because the work now spans more disciplines, the strongest PMs are increasingly hybrids. Method staffs and develops people in blended roles for exactly this reason.. Choose your hybrid lens . Every Resident learns the core product craft. Beyond that, you will lean into one of two hybrid paths, shaped by your interests and the product your team is building.. Product Strategy / Design Hybrid . A product strategist with design fluency, who shapes a proposition and proves it through design artifacts rather than long documents.. Turn research and stakeholder input into a clear product concept, then express it as journey maps, flows and clickable prototypes.. Use AI to go from interviews to a testable prototype quickly, and bring the craft and judgment to know when the result is good enough to put in front of users.. Work side by side with Design Residents on usability testing, and let what users do, not what a deck says, drive the next decision.. A good fit if you are curious about users, like making ideas tangible, and enjoy workshops and storytelling. . Product Strategy / Technology Hybrid . A product strategist with technology depth, who turns business intent into technically grounded direction and keeps feasibility honest.. Translate goals into well-formed requirements and user stories that engineers and AI coding tools can act on.. Build the technical fluency to discuss APIs, data, CI/CD and architecture trade-offs, and to plan scope, dependencies and capacity, including work done by AI agents.. Work with Engineering Residents to decide what to build, buy or skip, and to check what AI-assisted development produces.. A good fit if you are drawn to how things work under the hood, like structured problem solving, and want to speak credibly with engineers. . Not sure which one fits? That is normal. Tell us what you are drawn to in your application, and we will help you find your path during the program.. What you will do . You will contribute to a real product backlog, run light user research, and help make sure your team builds the right thing for the right users. Typical work includes:. Research and strategy. Combine market analysis, stakeholder interviews and AI-assisted synthesis to uncover product opportunities and test your hypotheses with real users.. Requirements and specs. Turn high-level goals and "fuzzy" concepts into precise requirements, user stories and context that both people and AI tools can build from.. Rapid prototyping. Use AI-assisted tools to turn ideas into prototypes or working demos, and put them in front of users early.. Agile delivery. Practice Agile principles with your team: backlog ownership, sprint rituals, and iterative, value-driven delivery.. Roadmapping and prioritization. Build outcome-focused roadmaps that balance vision, technical feasibility and constraints, and prioritize with evidence.. Verification and quality. Review AI-generated research, specs and code with a critical eye, and help define how the team will know the product works.. Storytelling and presentation. Turn complex data into clear narratives that win alignment, and present your team's progress and recommendations.. AI scouting. Explore where generative AI can speed up the team's workflow or unlock new value, and be honest about where it does not help.. What you will learn . The residency is built around three learning areas, with AI fluency woven through each.. Product management. Identifying needs, defining strategy, and coordinating teams to deliver valuable solutions across transformation, strategy and build.. Consulting. Analytical problem solving, clear communication and relationship skills used to deliver expert advice, including how to translate what AI can and cannot do into client value.. Leadership. Setting a clear vision, driving cross-functional alignment, and helping teams adopt new ways of working, including working effectively with AI agents.. Who we are looking for .
