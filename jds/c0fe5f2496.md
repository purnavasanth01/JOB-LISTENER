## Job description (verbatim)

Note: The job is a remote job and is open to candidates in USA. Dupple is hiring a Creative Content Intern to shape and improve content across its newsletters, websites, social media, courses and advertising. The role involves creating and editing content, evaluating AI-generated drafts, and turning editorial judgment into rules and examples that improve the company’s content systems.. . Responsibilities. Newsletters. Every day, our engine scans the news in tech, AI, security, finance and marketing, picks the stories and drafts our five daily editions. You decide whether it picked the right stories (and you will own the definition of what right means here!), whether each one is told well, and whether the subject line earns the open. When it falls short, you rewrite by hand, then turn the fix into examples and rules so the engine gets it right tomorrow. Websites. You make sure Toolradar, Dupple and our network of 11 other sites publish high-quality content that adds value to their readers. You work with the team and the engine that already writes for them to find formats our partners will like too. Instagram, X and LinkedIn: build an AI media brand. You study the best AI and tech accounts, work out what makes them grow, and build ours with Reels and carousels people watch to the end, save and send to friends. You own the plan, the format, the daily output and the growth, with Instagram first. On X and LinkedIn, you grow our reach with posts people save and share, not filler. AI Academy. You manage the freelancers who make our tutorials and courses, hold them to a high bar, and produce the tutorial videos (screen recordings with voiceover, cleanly edited). You also write the copy that sells the courses. Ad branding and copy, with our head of growth. Every week we launch a fresh batch of ads (static images, short videos and native-style posts). You own what the ads say and show: you bring the hooks, angles and visual ideas, often written by hand first, feed them into the builder, judge what it makes, and read the results with the head of growth each week to learn what makes a busy professional stop scrolling. . Skills. You love AI and tech, and you are optimistic about where they are going. You follow the news because you want to, not because it is your job. The hype excites you, and you can still tell a real breakthrough from noise. You curate with very high standards. Out of fifty stories, tools or ideas, you can pick the five worth a busy person's time, and say why the other forty-five did not make it. You have immaculate taste, and you can explain it. In design, media, tech products and education, you know what good looks like, and you can say exactly why something feels off. You are often on X. You usually know what happens before it makes the news. You think deeply. You ask why something worked before you copy it. You look for the pattern, not the one-off. You test constantly. You treat every format and idea as a hypothesis: you try new things all the time, ship small, and read the numbers without ego. Most tests will fail, and you are fine with that. You say what you think. If our work is weak, you tell us, kindly and clearly, with a better version. You change your mind when the data or a better argument says so. You are comfortable saying "I don't know.". Then you go and figure it out. You learn new tools and subjects fast. You use AI as a creative tool. You know when it makes the work better and when it makes it generic. You are excited to learn Claude Code, which you will use every week. You can be trusted with a lot. Nobody will watch your hours. You set your own priorities, switch between very different tasks in a day, and take pride in what ships. Your English is excellent and natural, written and spoken. You follow tech news from the US and Silicon Valley as closely as from the rest of the world, and you know what sounds natural to a global audience, and what sounds like a brand trying too hard. You enjoy working with a small international team, and a startup where your job changes as we grow. Already used Claude Code, or built their own workflows with AI tools. Grown an Instagram account (ideally in AI, tech or news), a newsletter or a website, and can tie specific creative decisions to the results. Worked at a media company, or a fast-growing startup. . Benefits. Profit share that grows with tenure.. Work from anywhere, with at least 3 hours of overlap each weekday with 9:00 to 18:00 Paris time..
