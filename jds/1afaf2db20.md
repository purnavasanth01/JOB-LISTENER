## Job description (verbatim)

Arthrex, Inc. is seeking a Technical Artist Intern to support its Medical Visualization team in developing interactive 3D applications for medical education and surgeon assistance. The intern will contribute to 3D asset creation, technical art workflows, visualization tools, and interactive experiences.. . Responsibilities. Explore emerging visualization, virtual reality (VR), and augmented reality (AR) technologies to support the development of interactive 3D applications. Create, refine, and optimize 3D assets, materials, textures, and shaders for products, anatomical structures, and virtual environments, including preparing and converting CAD models for real-time applications. Support AI-driven image and 3D asset generation workflows, content pipelines, and automation scripting efforts. Develop scripts and artist-facing tools to automate repetitive tasks, streamline 3D model preparation and asset processing, and improve artist workflows within real-time engines. Evaluate tools, plugins, and software development kits (SDKs) to support new features and document findings for team implementation. Design, prototype, and implement 2D and 3D user interfaces (UIs), motion graphics, and interactive visual elements for 3D environments, applying artistic skills to improve usability and visual quality. Manage and maintain digital assets within Unreal Engine and content management systems (CMS). Collaborate with artists, engineers, and other stakeholders to solve technical art challenges and enhance real-time visualization and interactive experiences. . Skills. An intern pursuing a degree in 3D Graphics, Engineering, Computer Science or Interactive Media. A college senior or graduate student pursuing a degree in 3D Graphics, Interactive Media, Computer Science, Engineering or a related field required. (Recent graduates must be within six months of their graduation date to qualify.). Strong technical aptitude with the ability to learn and apply new tools, workflows, and technologies in a fast-paced environment. Knowledge of CAD software is required. Strong 3D art fundamentals and experience with two or more of the following technologies is required: Unity, Unreal Engine, Autodesk Maya, Visual Studio, Omniverse, Rider, Pixyz, 3ds Max, or 3D graphics development. Knowledge of realistic 3D environment creation and scene dressing with proficiency in at least one of the following areas: programming, software testing, automation, scripting, 3D mathematics, or large language model (LLM) training. Be able to follow written and verbal direction. . Benefits. Medical insurance (if 25 or more hours per week), including access to telehealth services. Employee Assistance Program (EAP).
