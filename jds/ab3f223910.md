## Job description (verbatim)

IBM is hiring a Product Management Intern to help shape the user experience and offerings for IBM Quantum. The intern will research customer needs, inform roadmap priorities, iterate on product designs, and coordinate release planning with cross-functional teams.. . Responsibilities. Identify unmet or unknown needs of customers (e.g., computational chemists, optimization and quantum information researchers, enterprise R&D teams, quantum algorithm developers). Use quantitative analysis (e.g., usage data, job success rates, workload mix) and qualitative research (e.g., user interviews, support tickets, community forums). Scopes the impact of a problem, such as how many users it affects and what it costs them in time, QPU usage, or result quality. Recommend features or experiences to prioritize on the roadmap that support the team's success criteria and Objectives and Key Results (OKRs). Weigh customer value against technical constraints. Iterate on designs (e.g., APIs and SDK interfaces, workflow templates, documentation, results dashboards) with Design, Software Engineering, and Quantum Researchers. Collaborate with Product Marketing, Business Planning, Developer Relations, and Engineering to define release criteria (e.g., performance benchmarks against classical baselines, reliability thresholds, documentation readiness) and plans for customer acquisition and adoption. Build working knowledge of the quantum application stack (e.g., mapping a problem to a quantum algorithm, running circuits, error mitigation, classical post-processing). The goal is to translate accurately between what customers need and what the technology can do today. . Skills. Bachelor's Degree. Bachelors or higher degree in a STEM area.
