## Job description (verbatim)

SAP is hiring a Site Speed Performance Intern for its SAP.com Digital Platforms Team. The intern will analyze website performance, investigate front-end and crawler bottlenecks, and identify optimization opportunities across digital experiences. The role also supports AI-assisted testing and analysis workflows and coordinates with technical and business stakeholders.. . Responsibilities. Conduct synthetic and real-user performance testing using platforms such as Catchpoint and Akamai mPulse, and help evaluate future RUM capabilities. Analyze Core Web Vitals and related performance data using Chrome UX Report data and SAP.com Grafana dashboards to identify trends and regressions. Investigate front-end and rendering bottlenecks using Chrome DevTools and software-as-a-service (SaaS) performance platforms. Document findings and recommend optimization opportunities to product and development teams. Monitor Prerender.io bot traffic, rendering usage, and crawler performance for SAP.com and SAP.cn. Help analyze crawler errors, CDN routing rules, robots.txt directives, and custom user-agent traffic, and identify opportunities to reduce 404 responses. Support efforts to improve bot rendering efficiency and content accessibility. Support proof-of-concept projects designed to improve the performance of SAP crawlers and custom user-agent traffic. Explore SAP-approved generative AI and MCP-enabled tools for test-script generation, diagnostic analysis, and reporting. Identify opportunities to replace repetitive manual analysis with scalable, AI-assisted workflows. Review AI-generated findings and validate them against performance data. Collaborate with product owners, developers, UX teams, analysts, and external partners. Maintain performance documentation, dashboards, and internal knowledge resources.
