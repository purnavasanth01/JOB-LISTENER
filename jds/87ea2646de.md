Open up opportunities with HPE.. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Job Description: . . Job Summary: . The Cloud Security Engagement Intern supports the Public Cloud Security team in engaging business units, application teams, cloud platform owners, and other stakeholders on cloud security initiatives across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Working under the guidance of senior team members, the intern helps coordinate stakeholder outreach, track initiative progress, prepare communications, and maintain accurate initiative data. This role provides hands-on exposure to enterprise cloud security programs and to how security initiatives are planned, communicated, and adopted in a large organization.. . Responsibilities: . Stakeholder outreach support: Helps identify and document stakeholders, including business unit contacts, application owners, and cloud platform teams. Helps schedule and coordinate engagement meetings.. Initiative support: Assists with the rollout of cloud security initiatives, including foundational security controls, logging and monitoring, endpoint detection and response (EDR), and access management. Gathers information and follows up on action items.. Communications: Drafts meeting notes, status updates, emails, frequently asked questions (FAQs), and presentation slides for review by senior team members.. Tracking and reporting: Maintains accurate engagement trackers and inventories of subscriptions, accounts, and projects. Collects data and helps prepare metrics and dashboards that show initiative progress.. Issue logging: Records stakeholder questions, blockers, risks, and dependencies, and routes them to the appropriate team member for resolution. Tracks open items and follows up on their status.. Research: Researches cloud security best practices, cloud provider security features, and industry frameworks to support team planning and guidance materials.. Learning and development: Attends team meetings and stakeholder sessions to build knowledge of cloud security, program management, and stakeholder engagement. Completes an intern project and presents the results to the team.. . Education and Experience Required: . Currently pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in computer science, information systems, cybersecurity, business, or a related field.. No prior professional experience is required. Coursework, academic projects, or extracurricular activities in cybersecurity, cloud computing, or project coordination are a plus.. Entry-level certifications, such as AWS Cloud Practitioner, Microsoft Azure Fundamentals, Google Cloud Digital Leader, or CompTIA Security+, are preferred but not required.. . Knowledge and Skills: . Basic understanding of cloud computing concepts and at least one public cloud platform: AWS, Azure, or GCP.. Foundational knowledge of cybersecurity concepts, including identity and access management, logging and monitoring, and vulnerability management.. Strong written and verbal communication skills.. Strong organizational skills and attention to detail.. Ability to work effectively with both technical and non-technical team members.. Curiosity, willingness to learn, and a proactive approach to organizing and following up on tasks.. Proficiency with Microsoft Office tools, including Excel, PowerPoint, Word, and Teams; familiarity with SharePoint, Jira, or ServiceNow is a plus.. . Internship Success Measures: . Engagement trackers and inventories are accurate, complete, and up to date.. Communications, meeting notes, and status updates are clear, accurate, and completed on time.. Stakeholder action items, open issues, and dependencies are followed up on consistently..
