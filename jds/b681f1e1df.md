Start & End dates Wednesday, June 2 2027 - Friday, August 20, 2027 Monday, June 14, 2027 - Friday, September 3, 2027 About the Role: The Insider Risk Engineering Intern is a member of CrowdStrike's Global Security team and supports the Insider Risk Program across both its analytical and technical work. This is a hybrid role for a student who wants to understand how a corporate insider risk program actually runs — and who wants to help build the tooling and reporting that make it run better. You will spend your time on workflow automation, program reporting and dashboards, integration testing, and analytical projects that support the team's priorities. On the engineering side, you will write scripts and build automation that reduce manual steps in intake, triage support, and evidence handling; build and improve dashboards that track program workload, cycle time, and performance; test new tool integrations; and recommend improvements to Insider Risk training materials. Alongside that build work, you will develop core analytical tradecraft: walking a synthetic case from intake to a mock report, learning to separate facts, inferences, and unknowns while documenting sources, meeting with colleagues across threat intelligence, physical security, GRC, HR, and Legal, and understanding why insider risk monitoring is deliberately scoped, reviewed, and governed by privacy and need-to-know principles. You will close the internship by presenting your work and its measured impact to security leadership. We are looking for a creative, collaborative, and motivated student in computer science, cybersecurity, data science, or a related field, with scripting experience and an interest in insider risk, investigations, counterintelligence, risk management, and national security. What You'll Do: Automation and workflow build-out: Build scripts and workflow automation that reduce manual steps in intake, triage support, and evidence handling. Analyst judgment stays with analysts. Reporting and dashboard support: Support program reporting and dashboards, such as workload, cycle time, and detection performance, using aggregated or de-identified data where possible. Integration testing: Test new integrations and changes to tooling, and document results and defects. Documentation and hand-off: Document what you build so the work survives the academic-year hand-offs. Training material: Review current Insider Risk training and identify potential improvements. Briefings: Shadow and conduct counterintelligence briefings for key CrowdStrikers to develop briefing skills. What You'll Need: Must be currently enrolled and completed at least 2 years at a 4-year university by the start of the internship, working towards a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree and graduating between December 2027 - December 2028.
