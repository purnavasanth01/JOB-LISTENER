## Job description (verbatim)

The Data Analytics internship offers a hands-on opportunity to contribute to the development of a robust data model that supports a key business domain within our organization. The intern will work closely with the Analytics and Data Engineering team to understand business requirements, explore existing data sources, and help design and implement scalable data pipelines and models. The Mom's Meals Summer InternshipProgram is a paid 12-week program. Our project-based internship program provides participants with the training, coaching, and hands-on experience to be successful professionals in their chosen fields. Our internship assignments focus on practical, value-added projects that help create a connection between what is learned in the classroom and what is required in the manufacturing and healthcare sectors. Candidates who progress to a final interview will be asked to provide examples of data-related projects will be requested for review.
