## Job description (verbatim)

About the role . . We are seeking an intern to help secure an enterprise AI capability built for senior executive leadership. Because it will touch highly sensitive information, identity, data protection and AI-specific threat testing are core to whether it can go live. You'll work alongside Global Cybersecurity specialists and the AI engineering and data interns to design controls in from the start. . . What you will be responsible for . Identity and access - design need-to-know entitlements for executive-level and material non-public data. . Data protection - support data classification, loss prevention, encryption, and logging and audit trails. . Third-party risk - help review AI services and connectors against security requirements. . AI threat modeling - threat model an AI agent targeted at executives, covering prompt injection, data leakage and misuse. . Red-team testing - build and run tests for prompt injection, jailbreaks and cross-boundary data leakage, document results and recommend mitigations. . Secure configuration and response - review connector configuration and help draft an incident response playbook. . Hands-on experience with large language model (LLM) applications , such as LLM APIs or frameworks (Azure OpenAI, Amazon Bedrock, LangChain, Semantic Kernel or LlamaIndex), including a working understanding of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), embeddings and how agents decide when to use tools. . Awareness of LLM security and reliability risks , including hallucination, prompt injection through ingested content and data leakage across access boundaries, with experience testing guardrails and failure modes and recommending fixes. . . Every pod member wears more than one hat . You'll work in a small, cross-functional pod alongside interns in AI engineering, data, product, governance and cybersecurity. Depending on your strengths, you may also: . Advise the AI engineering interns on guardrails and secure design patterns. . Support the governance seat on audit and regulatory evidence. . Help test secure data ingestion into Databricks. . . What we value . Pursuing a degree in Cybersecurity, or in Computer Science, Information Systems or a related field with a Cybersecurity concentration. . Strong grounding in security fundamentals: identity and access management, encryption, logging, network and application security. . Scripting ability in Python, PowerShell or similar. . Interest in AI security, including LLM-specific threats such as prompt injection and data exfiltration. . Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's, Master's or PhD program. Underclassmen are welcome to apply. . Available January to June 2027 for at least 30 hours per week, including working onsite 4 days per week. . Clear written and verbal communication, including explaining technical work to non-technical and senior audiences. . Curiosity, ownership and comfort working with ambiguity in a highly regulated environment. . . Preferred . Hands-on AI or LLM experience, or familiarity with the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications or MITRE ATLAS. . Coursework in cyber ethics, law or regulation. . Security certifications (such as Security+) or CTF experience. . . When applying, please have hands-on cybersecurity experience through labs, competitions (such as CTFs), research, internships or projects. Coursework alone is not enough. . . .
