Position Summary: As an intern in our Advanced Computing Lab, you will work with a team of electrical engineers and computer science researchers on analyzing and improving mission system performance metrics through the use of novel AI-focused hardware accelerators, FPGAs, optimized low-level algorithms, and experimental compilers. Duties: Complete tasks to support ongoing projects within the Advanced Computing Lab, which could consist of Profiling performance of various compute systems Testing and debugging ML-related algorithms Supporting the development of prototypes, tools, and demos Collecting and curating project-related code, research publications, and hardware specifications FPGA design and simulation Outdoor data-collection fieldwork that requires the physical capability to carry moderately heavy equipment (approx. 25–40 lbs) at various field sites Requirements: Current enrollment in an undergraduate or graduate degree granting program. Able to work an internship schedule during the summer semester. Able to work on-site in the SEI Pittsburgh office. Eligibility to work in the United States without visa sponsorship.
