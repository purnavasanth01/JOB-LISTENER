Ralph Lauren is seeking a Digital Site Content Intern to support site content strategy and execution across its brands. The intern will assist with seasonal content planning, product and asset checks, technical briefs, user acceptance testing, and coordination of site launches.. . Responsibilities. Contribute to seasonal Digital Site Content strategies by brainstorming content ideas with managers and associates and building presentation decks. Partner with associates to complete technical briefs and conduct user acceptance testing (UAT) on site content pages before launch on RLE. Review shot lists for upcoming shoots to confirm that key products and categories are represented and that RLE has ownership of featured products. Collaborate with Visual Merchandising and Merchandising Operations to brief curated product grids and track product readiness ahead of launches. Support associates in completing Monthly Site Content Briefs, including wireframes for upcoming site pages. Research other brands’ site experiences and present competitive findings and content opportunities to the Site Content team. . Skills.
