Job Description . Hybrid/Remote: This role is categorized as Hybrid and will be based in our Warren, MI office. . . Role: As the Cybersecurity Education & Awareness Program Intern, you will help shape how our employees learn to recognize and respond to emerging cyber threats. You will work alongside the Cybersecurity Education & Awareness team to design, build and measure training content, phishing simulations and awareness campaigns that reach thousands of employees. . Your internship will center on an AI-forward capstone project: rethinking a traditional awareness approach and prototyping an AI-powered solution that is more personal, more timely and more measurable. You will finish the summer by presenting your prototype, results and a recommendation for scale to security leadership. . . About the Team: The Cybersecurity Education & Awareness team, part of GM's Corporate Cybersecurity team, builds the human layer of our defense. We run enterprise security training, phishing simulation, awareness campaigns and targeted programs for high-risk roles. We partner closely with Security Operations, Threat Intelligence, Privacy, HR, Communications and IT. . . What You'll Do: . Lead the AI-forward capstone project from problem definition through pilot, measurement and executive readout . Design and develop engaging awareness content — micro-learning modules, videos, infographics, newsletters and intranet posts — using AI tools to accelerate creation . Support phishing simulation campaigns, from scenario design to results analysis and follow-up coaching . Analyze program data to identify trends and turn them into actionable insights . Help plan and promote Cybersecurity Awareness Month and other enterprise campaigns and events . Collaborate with Security Operations, Threat Intelligence, IT and Communications to align messaging and learn how to build relationship . . Your Skills & Abilities: . Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Cybersecurity, Information Systems, Computer Science, Instructional Design, Communications, or a related field . Strong written and visual communication skills, with the ability to make technical topics clear and engaging for any audience . Foundational knowledge of cybersecurity concepts such as phishing, social engineering, password and MFA, and data protection . Curiosity about human behavior and what motivates people to change habits . Hands-on experience using generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini) to research, create content or build simple workflows . Self-starter who can operate under ambiguity and manage a project independently in a hybrid or remote setting . . What You'll Gain: . Knowledge and Hands-on experience at the intersection of cybersecurity, AI and human behavior . Ownership of a real, AI-forward project with visibility to security leadership . Mentorship from security awareness, threat and AI practitioners . A portfolio-ready capstone you can showcase in future roles . .
