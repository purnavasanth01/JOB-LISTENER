## Job description (verbatim)

The Tech Interactive is hiring a STEM Impact & Data Reporting Intern to support evaluation of its STEM programs by aggregating, analyzing, and visualizing quantitative and qualitative data. The intern will help maintain reporting workflows, test data tools, participate in quarterly reviews, and develop a data science resource for educators.. . Responsibilities. Aggregate and update key quantitative and qualitative performance indicators in preparation for quarterly data reviews. Design and maintain user-friendly dashboards and spreadsheets that summarize complex survey results into clear charts, tables, and visual summaries tailored for varied stakeholders. Assist with all stages of data administration, including raw data entry, cleaning, validation, and standardizing database records. Develop a data science resource which includes a professional snapshot of your experiences, vocabulary, and a hands-on activity, expanding on The Tech's published online education resources. Collaborate with the team to identify gaps in existing data science resources for educators and test the usability of the newly developed activity. Experiment with and evaluate modern data-science platforms, visualization software, and reporting tools to optimize internal non-profit data workflows. Actively participate in two full cycles of the non-profit's quarterly data review process to track trends and present actionable insights. . Skills. Must be a current undergraduate pursuing a degree in Data Science, Statistics, Information Management, Education/STEM, Social Sciences, Public Policy, Non-Profit Management, or a related field. Familiarity with spreadsheets (Excel, Google Sheets) including pivot tables, formulas, and chart design. Strong attention to detail with an interest in working with both numbers (quantitative metrics) and narrative feedback (qualitative survey responses). Ability to present data insights clearly to non-technical team members and collaborate effectively. Alignment with The Tech's mission to inspire the innovator in everyone and expand global access to quality STEM education. Exposure to data visualization or analysis tools (e.g., Data Studio, Tableau, Power BI, R, Python) is a plus. . Benefits. On-site role with some flexibility; flexibility will vary depending on the needs of the project.. . Company Overview. The Tech Interactive is a science and technology center for the development of educational content for students.
