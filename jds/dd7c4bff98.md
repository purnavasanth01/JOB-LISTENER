Do it Best is a home improvement industry cooperative supporting independently owned member stores. The User Experience Intern will support the UX team by contributing to research, journey mapping, interface design, information architecture audits, and documentation that improve digital tools and onboarding experiences.. . Skills. No relocation is offered, candidates must be local to the Fort Wayne, Indiana area for the Summer 2027 timeframe. Experience with design and collaboration tools such as Figma, Miro, or similar (coursework or project-based). Familiarity with UX research methods and usability principles. Strong analytical and visual communication skills. Detail-oriented, organized, and eager to learn in a fast-paced environment. . Benefits.
