Schneider Electric is hiring a Digital Services Engineering Intern to prepare graduating students for a career as a Digital Services Engineer. Responsibilities. Learn about the range of products, software, and services provided by Schneider Electric Digital Services team. Partner with experienced Engineers to gain experience leading initiatives, setting objectives, developing execution plans, collaborating, and influencing stakeholders, and delivering results. Collaborate with a team of Power Management and Digital Services experts to support customer engagement and improve customer experience. Learn to digitize technical articles to troubleshoot software and products to simplify customer's self-serve ability to deliver on the end user's power management and sustainability KPIs. Help customers maintain the reliability and performance of their complete solution and address any potential technical problems in their server, network, operating system, software, or connected devices.
