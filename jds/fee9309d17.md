Arthrex is seeking a Technical Artist Intern to support its Medical Visualization team in developing interactive 3D applications for medical education and product visualization. The intern will create and manage 3D assets, explore emerging technologies, and contribute to visualization workflows and content pipelines.. . Responsibilities. Explore emerging visualization, virtual reality (VR), and augmented reality (AR) technologies to support the development of interactive 3D applications. Create and refine materials, textures, and shaders for anatomical models, products, and virtual environments. Organize, maintain, and optimize 3D asset and product libraries across multiple platforms and applications. Evaluate tools, plugins, and software development kits (SDKs) to support new features and document findings for team implementation. Develop high-quality 3D visualizations of products, anatomical structures, environments, and virtual production sets. Support AI-driven image and 3D asset generation workflows, content pipelines, and automation scripting efforts. Manage and maintain digital assets within Unity, Unreal Engine, and content management systems (CMS). Collaborate with cross-functional teams to enhance real-time visualization, simulation, and interactive experiences. . Skills. A college senior or graduate student pursuing a degree in 3D Graphics, Interactive Media, Computer Science, Engineering or a related field required. Knowledge of one or more of the following areas is required: programming, software testing, automation, scripting, 3D mathematics, or large language model (LLM) training.
