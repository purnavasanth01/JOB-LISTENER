Vizient’s Research Analytics & Insights team uses data and research methods to help healthcare organizations understand performance, quality outcomes, and emerging industry questions. The intern will contribute to a healthcare analytics project by developing an analytic question, researching relevant literature and data, conducting analyses, and presenting findings.. . Responsibilities. Complete training in the Vizient CDB online tool and use its front-end interface to support healthcare data analysis. Contribute to a defined research or analytics project addressing a RAIN team priority or client-relevant healthcare question. Conduct focused literature and background research, refine analytic questions, and identify appropriate measures and datasets. Perform data extraction, validation, descriptive analysis, and interpretation to identify meaningful trends and insights. Collaborate with RAIN colleagues and cross-functional partners to review methods, findings, and implications. Develop clear documentation, visualizations, and a final presentation or written research deliverable for technical and nontechnical audiences. Support updates to internal SharePoint content and team knowledge resources, as needed. . Skills. Currently pursuing a master's or doctoral degree in health services research, public health, epidemiology, biostatistics, data science, health informatics, healthcare administration, or a related quantitative field; an undergraduate degree must be completed before the internship begins.
