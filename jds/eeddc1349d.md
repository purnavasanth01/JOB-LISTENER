## Job description (verbatim)

Job Description The Data and AI Enablement Intern will join the Constellation Brands Data & Analytics Platform team to help advance the adoption of modern data, analytics, and AI capabilities across the enterprise. Working closely with data engineers, platform engineers, architects, analysts, and business stakeholders, the intern will gain hands-on experience with Microsoft Fabric, including OneLake, Gen2 Dataflows, Data Pipelines, semantic models, Power BI, Copilot, and Fabric Data Agents. The role will provide practical exposure to the full data lifecycle, from ingesting and transforming data through building governed semantic models and analytics experiences, to exploring how generative AI and Fabric Data Agents can enable natural-language interaction with trusted enterprise data. The ideal candidate is curious about data and AI, enjoys solving technical problems, and is eager to learn how modern cloud data platforms are designed, governed, and operated in a large enterprise. Responsibilities: Work with technical mentors on strategic Data & Analytics Platform initiatives supporting Microsoft Fabric and enterprise AI enablement. Assist with the development and testing of Microsoft Fabric Lakehouses, including organizing data within OneLake and working with Delta-based data structures. Build and enhance Fabric Gen2 Dataflows and Fabric Data Pipelines to ingest, transform, and orchestrate data from enterprise data sources. Assist with designing and maintaining Power BI semantic models, including relationships, measures, business definitions, and reusable analytics components. Develop and enhance Power BI reports and dashboards that provide intuitive, actionable insights to business users. Explore and test Microsoft Fabric Copilot capabilities across data engineering, analytics, semantic modeling, and Power BI scenarios. Assist with devleloping Fabric Data Agents that enable users to interact with governed enterprise data through natural-language questions. Provide ad hoc data analysis, technical documentation, presentation, and project support as needed. Participate in Constellation Brands internship activities, including senior leader meetings, goal setting, networking, team-building activities, tours, and a final internship presentation, consistent with the existing internship program structure. Core Competencies to be Successful: Technical and problem-solving skills. Ability to effectively communicate and organize across levels and departments. Strong ability to concisely and logically deliver analyses and proposals. Strong initiative to seek out information and solutions. Strong focus on quality and timely work. Excellent verbal and written communication skills. Qualifications: Current student in, or recent graduate of, a Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or other STEM-related program. Basic understanding of AI concepts, including generative AI and common limitations/risks (e.g., hallucinations, bias, privacy considerations). Familiarity with productivity and analytics tools (e.g., Excel), and exposure to data querying languages such as SQL; familiarity with Python or similar scripting tools is a plus. Ability to work independently and to exercise strong technical judgment. Excellent problem solving and trouble-shooting skills. ADA Physical/Mental/Workplace Requirements Occasional lifting up to 25 lbs. Sitting, working at desk/personal computer for extended periods of time. Primary work environment is within a corporate office setting. Ability to travel commercially and internationally.
