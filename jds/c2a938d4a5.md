## Job description (verbatim)

Ralph Lauren is hiring a Women's Polo Sweaters Product Management Intern to support the Product Management team throughout the seasonal product lifecycle. The intern will help manage product development, production and sample tracking, costing, deadlines, and coordination with cross-functional teams and sourcing offices.. . Responsibilities. The Women's Polo Sweaters Product Management Intern will provide day-to-day support to the Product Management team throughout the seasonal product lifecycle. Assist with sample tracking, BOMs, system updates, costing documentation, approvals, and production follow-up to help ensure information is accurate and key development milestones stay on track. Maintain BOMs and technical packages in PLM for Mainline, PFS, and RLX/Golf. Update development trackers, WIP charts, calendars, delivery dates, and open issues. Organize development files and samples, including yarns, trims, lab dips, costing, and approvals. Maintain trim and lab dip charts and track submissions and approvals. Distribute technical packages to vendors and maintain required PLM updates. Update BOMs for line changes, add/drops, and development changes. Attend fittings as needed and track samples and fit comments. Track artwork, embroidery, prints, lab dips, embellishments, and other development submissions. Follow up with sourcing offices and internal teams on outstanding samples, approvals, and information. Assist with seasonal production and order tracking in GFE, PLM, SAP, and o9. Maintain TNA, OTB, unit, and delivery trackers as directed by the Manager. Track Bulk and SRS orders and follow up on outstanding information. Coordinate SRS and showroom samples with sourcing offices, vendors, and Merchandising. Check in, organize, and distribute samples and maintain accurate sample records. Assist with PO-related activities, including PIRS, PRS, UPC, and sizing information. Support Chase and Reconciliation activities by maintaining trackers and updating information. Track costing submissions and help ensure cost sheets are complete in PLM/GFE. Prepare cost BOM snapshots and other costing information as needed. Communicate key deadlines to sourcing offices and cross-functional teams. Track outstanding development, sample, approval, OTB, and delivery actions. Flag delays or concerns to the Manager for follow-up. Maintain day-to-day communication with Design, Production, PMT, Technical Design, Merchandising, Operations, and sourcing offices. Schedule meetings, prepare agendas, and track follow-up actions as needed. . Skills.
