About Vital Lyfe Vital Lyfe is a tech company redefining water autonomy through innovation, creating a new category of personal water making technology built to scale where infrastructure can’t. The Mission We're looking for a Software Engineering Intern to help build the software behind a high-demand consumer hardware product. We're spinning up manufacturing from scratch, scaling fast, and the software you work on will directly affect how we build, track, and deliver thousands of units. You'll work alongside senior engineers on the tools, data systems, and infrastructure that power our operations and help us scale. What You'll Do • Build and improve internal tools that give our engineering and operations teams real-time visibility into production and manufacturing workflows • Write integrations and scripts that connect systems and keep data reliable across the company • Design and maintain dashboards that help teams understand operational data and make better decisions • Assist with backend and cloud infrastructure work as we continue scaling our services • Debug issues, write tests, and ship improvements alongside senior engineers What We're Looking For • Strong programming fundamentals and comfort learning new systems quickly • Experience with at least one modern programming language such as Python or TypeScript • Interest in software that supports real-world products and operations • Familiarity with APIs, web applications, or cloud-based systems • Practical and curious, with a habit of taking ownership and following through • Able to break down a problem, work through it, and know when to ask for help • Excited to work in a fast-paced environment where software and hardware intersect Nice to Have • Experience supporting or contributing to live backend systems • Background in building user-facing interfaces with React or a similar frontend framework • Exposure to SQL, structured data formats, and dashboards or data visualization tools • Familiarity with AWS or cloud infrastructure • Interest in manufacturing, hardware-software integration, or IoT systems Physical Job Requirements - Essential Functions - Hands on work on development hardware. - Lift and carry up to 50 lbs. unassisted - Work involves standing, crouching, and walking for extended periods.
