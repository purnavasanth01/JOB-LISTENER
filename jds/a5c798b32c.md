## Job description (verbatim)

Bain & Company is a global consultancy that helps ambitious organizations define the future and achieve extraordinary results. The Product Management & Innovation Intern will work on cross-functional teams to identify innovation opportunities, design and prototype digital products, and contribute to product strategy, roadmaps, and launches. The role involves applying AI, data platforms, automation, agile methods, and human-centered design to address real business challenges.. . Responsibilities. Identify and validate opportunities for innovation using human-centered design methods. Translate user and business needs into product concepts, prototypes, and MVPs. Contribute to product vision, strategy, and roadmap development for digital initiatives. Partner with engineers, designers, and clients to test and iterate solutions rapidly. Leverage emerging technologies — including AI, data platforms, and automation — to accelerate business outcomes. Support agile ceremonies, define user stories, and help manage backlogs across cross-functional teams. Support backlog & planning (story writing, acceptance criteria), contributing to lightweight PRDs and simple roadmap artifacts. Document and share internship learnings to senior Bain leaders. . Skills. This role requires in-person collaboration. You'll need to **co-locate with your team at least three days a week**, whether that's in a Bain office or at a client site, which may require travel. * Prior experience in **product management**, startups, consulting, or technology. * Coursework from a related **dual-degree program** (e.g., engineering, computer science, design, or product management). * Ability to use **Gen-AI powered product development tools** effectively (e.g., Cursor, Synthetic Users, V0, Loveable, etc.). .
