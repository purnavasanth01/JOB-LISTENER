ABOUT STRAVA Strava is the app for active people. With over 200 million athletes in more than 185 countries, it’s more than tracking workouts—it’s where people make progress together, from new habits to new personal bests. No matter your sport or how you track it, Strava’s got you covered. Find your crew, crush your goals, and make every effort count. Start your journey https://www.strava.com/subscription with Strava today. Our mission is simple: to motivate people to live their best active lives. We believe in the power of movement to connect and drive people forward. As an Engineering Intern at Strava, you will be embedded in a team working on real projects that ship to our athletes and within the company, treated like a full-time member of the engineering team. You'll collaborate with cross functional teams that help us support and deliver to our athletes faster, while keeping them inspired and motivated. We’ll give you experienced mentors and all the tools you need to make a meaningful impact on our product. To see examples of what past interns have contributed, read our Engineering Blog https://medium.com/strava-engineering/tagged/internships! AVAILABLE INTERNSHIP: Strava is looking for software engineering interns for a range of disciplines. Strava's engineering teams build and run the apps and services that millions of athletes rely on every day. Our engineers work across iOS, Android, web, and backend systems, shipping to a global community through frequent releases and experimentation, in close partnership with product, design, and data teams. This internship will be supporting the User Lifecycle team, which helps athletes build lasting habits and connections on Strava. The team owns registration and onboarding, the home feed, streaks, recaps like Month in Sport and Year in Sport, and gamified experiences like segments and challenges, working across mobile and backend services to build, test, and ship improvements. We follow a flexible hybrid model that requires you in our SF office four days per week. WHAT YOU’LL DO: - Ship to Millions of Athletes: Build and ship features across mobile, web, and backend services that reach athletes on the surfaces they use every day, including the home feed, onboarding and registration, and streaks. - Improve the First Impression: Help new athletes get set up and find value faster by working on the first-time user experience, registration, and sign-in flows, and measure the impact through A/B experiments. - Bring Data to Life: Work on shareable, personalized experiences like Month in Sport, connecting backend data pipelines and APIs to polished, animated client experiences. - Make Segments and Challenges More Engaging: Contribute to features that make leaderboards, segments, and other motivating games easier to discover and play. - Build for Scale and Reliability: Learn how to ship safely at scale with feature flags, monitoring, code review, and phased rollouts, and help modernize parts of a large, long-lived codebase. - Collaborate Across Disciplines: Partner with product managers, designers, analysts, and engineers across platforms, and learn from experienced mentors through design review, code review, and experiment readouts. WE’RE EXCITED ABOUT YOU BECAUSE: - You're eager to build features end to end, from technical design through to shipping and measuring real-world impact. - You have hands-on experience with at least one modern programming language (such as Kotlin, Swift, Scala, Go, or Ruby) and have built projects through coursework, internships, or personal work. - You have a solid grasp of computer science fundamentals, including data structures, algorithms, and object-oriented or functional design. - You write clean, well-testedf code and welcome feedback and mentorship as part of your growth. - You care about the athlete experience and think about how design, data, and performance come together in a product people use daily. - You bring curiosity, ask thoughtful questions, and contribute to a collaborative and inclusive team culture. - Bonus: experience with mobile development, REST APIs or backend services, A/B testing, or analytics. - Are currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, with an expected graduation date of May 2027, December 2027, May 2028 — OR – have recently completed or are currently enrolled in a coding bootcamp, technical training program, or are transitioning into software engineering through equivalent practical experience. THE SUMMER INTERNSHIP PROGRAM: - The program will be in our San Francisco, CA office with a hybrid office attendance requirement of 4 days per week - Housing Stipend for Summer is provided to live in SF for 3 months - Length of program: 10-12 weeks (Summer 2027 roughly June through August). - Working Hours: 40 hours per week - This is a paid internship We will be closing applications for this role on Fri, Nov 27 at 5:00pm PST.
