Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all . . Job Description . As a PFA Engineer Intern on the Yield Enhancement (YE) team, you will support physical failure analysis investigations of defect issues found by YE and Fab customers. You will learn to isolate, expose, and image physical defects in advanced memory devices, analyze the results to help find the root cause, and present clear findings to engineering partners. Alongside your daily work in the lab, you will complete a focused improvement project during the 3-month internship and present the results at the end. You will set your priorities following the YE PFA queue, with guidance from a mentor engineer.. . Responsibilities and Tasks . Physical Failure Analysis and Investigation . Performs PFA sample preparation and analysis (e.g., deprocessing/delayering, cross-sectioning, FIB, SEM/TEM imaging) according to area skill guidelines.. Reviews EFA results, fault isolation data, and probe/yield data to decide where to target the physical analysis.. Identifies and categorizes physical defect signatures using defect references, and helps characterize new or unknown defect types.. Performs data analysis to back up PFA results in pursuit of root cause.. Summarizes findings in clear, concise reports (images, defect location, likely root cause) and communicates them to part owners and YE/Fab engineers.. Lab Equipment Operation . Learns to operate PFA lab equipment safely and efficiently, following defined safety and contamination-control guidelines.. Builds an understanding of how the equipment works and supports basic troubleshooting with equipment owners.. Internship Project and Continuous Improvement . Owns a focused, short-term project that fits a 3-month timeline and presents the outcome to the team and leadership at the end of the internship.. Completes all required safety and equipment training certifications defined by area guidelines.. Builds technical knowledge of device structure, process flow, and failure mechanisms for current and future parts.. Suggests improvements for the department and participates in team projects that support department and company objectives.. AI-Enabled Analysis and Productivity . Uses approved AI and digital productivity tools (e.g., Microsoft Copilot) to work more efficiently on data analysis, image/report generation, knowledge retrieval, and problem-solving.. Uses AI tools to speed up documentation, communication, and investigation workflows while keeping data accurate and applying engineering judgment.. Uses AI technologies responsibly, following company policies, data protection requirements, and intellectual property safeguards.. Schedule . 3-month full-time internship, day shift (Monday–Friday).. Education . Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Materials Science & Engineering, Physics, Chemical Engineering, or a related field.. Qualifications . Basic understanding of semiconductor devices and fabrication processes.. Hands-on lab experience is a plus (e.g., microscopy, SEM/FIB, sample preparation, cleanroom).. Strong attention to detail and good analytical and problem-solving skills.. Clear written and verbal communication; able to summarize technical results simply.. Familiarity with data tools (Excel, JMP, Python, etc.) is preferred.. Experience . No prior industry experience required. Previous internships or research projects in semiconductor or failure analysis are preferred.. . .
