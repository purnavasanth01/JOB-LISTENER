Overview . . Hussmann's Internship Program provides meaningful, real-life experiences while working as a solutions leader in the food retailing industry. Hussmann interns receive competitive compensation, participate in community stewardship, network with company leadership, and learn about career opportunities available at Hussmann. . . As a Product Management Intern in the 2027 Hussmann Summer Internship Program, you will gain experience and insight into the day in the life of a product development professional. You will work across the team to provide product development support and work to market Hussmann’s products and services. . . Responsibilities . . Gain hands on experience in product management including participating in a variety of projects, meetings, and trainings. . Gain exposure to the product development process from initiation to launch. . Draft project initiations, business cases, and product requirements for new product requests. . Work with the marketing communications team on promoting Hussmann’s products. . Develop tools and analytics that create efficiencies in our department using Artificial Intelligence. . Utilize AI to analyze market trends, market sizing, competitive assessments, etc. . Network with senior leaders, associates, and other program interns to learn about our organization. . Tour Hussmann's 1.6 million square foot manufacturing facility, our Global Hub for hosting customers, and the R&D Lab where we test product quality. . Participate in community stewardship outreach, career development events, and other team-building offsite events. . Compete in the annual Ideation & Innovation Challenge. . Ideation & Innovation Challenge : Throughout the twelve-week program, all Hussmann interns will divide into cross-functional teams and compete with one another for the Hussmann Ideation & Innovation Challenge. Working with Hussmann mentors and leaders, you and your team will identify a solution to a provided problem statement. At the end of the summer, you’ll present your team’s business proposal to a panel of judges, executive leaders, and the organization – and possibly create a roadmap for Hussmann’s next great innovation. . . Qualifications . . Currently enrolled in school pursuing a minimum of a bachelor’s degree . Minimum 3.0 GPA . Strong analytical and problem-solving skills . Excellent interpersonal and communication skills . Ability to effectively interact with all levels of the organization . Ability to set priorities, manage multiple projects, and demonstrate strong attention to detail . Highly motivated and eager to learn . Strong computer skills . Ability to relocate, if not local . The summer internship will run from the third week of May through the first week of August. These dates may be changed to meet the needs of the student. . . . About Hussmann . With a nationwide network of skilled technicians, Hussmann goes beyond manufacturing — a trusted partner, relentlessly driving customer success. . .
