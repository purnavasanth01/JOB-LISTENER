Provide data analytics and statistics support on Internal Audit and process improvement projects. Design, develop, and maintain end-to-end analytics workflows in Microsoft Fabric, including data ingestion, transformation, modeling, and consumption, to support continuous monitoring and audit analytics. Help develop and implement continuous monitoring / auditing software solutions to manage high-risk areas. Take part in investigative activities and translate them into actionable business intelligence. Learn how real-world business data is sourced, stored, cleaned, and integrated across multiple systems, and how these datasets come together in Microsoft Fabric to support enterprise analytics. MA/MS in a quantitative (Mathematics, Statistics, Economics) or technical (e.g. Computer Science) field. Experience working with SQL, Python, Power BI, Excel (or other similar tools).
