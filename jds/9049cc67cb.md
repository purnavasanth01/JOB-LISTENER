Join the curious. . . As a Product Management Co-op, you will spend six months working 20 hours per week on the Bridges team, working directly with the Senior Director of Product for Activate. You will own real pieces of the roadmap, run experiments, and ship to customers on the web and in the Ancestry app. You will work with product managers, designers, engineers, data scientists, DNA scientists, and user researchers. . This role is designed as a path into product management at Ancestry. Co-ops who perform strongly will be considered for a full-time product role. . . What you'll do: . Understand DNA-first customers. Dig into research, customer feedback, and session recordings to learn why DNA customers do or don't move into family history, and where they drop off. . Shape and ship Bridges experiences. Help define features that connect DNA matches, origins, and grandparent-level insights to tree building and records, then write clear requirements and work with design and engineering to ship them. . Run experiments. Form hypotheses, set up A/B tests with analytics partners, analyze the results, and recommend next steps. . Improve the path to subscription. Find ways to make the moment a DNA customer meets a paywall clearer and more relevant to what they care about. . Measure what matters. Track Bridges metrics such as DNA-to-family-history conversion, tree creation and repeat visits, and share what you learn with the team and leadership. . Build with AI. Use our AI tools for discovery, prototyping, and requirements, and take part in the product org's AI Builder Weeks. . . Who you are: .
