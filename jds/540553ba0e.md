Interns will work on specific projects, designed to help them gain real-world, hands-on experience working independently under an assigned supervisor and technical mentor.. . Primary Purpose: . The Data Analyst is responsible for providing analytical support in the PowerFolio group for Power related transactions. Data Analyst works closely with PowerFolio Commodity Services managers and directors, sales, and structuring desks in supporting the execution of complex commodity transactions, maintaining customer PowerFolio web sites regarding data, risk valuation models, and working with customer care in the generation of Monthly Results Reports. In addition, Data Analyst are also responsible for managing and tracking customer transaction addenda, triggers and contracts and any changes made to these documents/contracts.. .
