## Job description (verbatim)

Parspec is seeking a Product Management Intern to work with product leadership on its AI quoting agent for distributors and rep agencies. The intern will conduct customer discovery, define and prototype agent workflows, write engineering-ready specifications, establish reliability and success criteria, and analyze pricing models.. . Responsibilities. Interview distributor and rep agency users to understand how quotes get built today, step by step, including the workarounds and judgment calls that never show up in a process diagram. Map the quoting workflow end to end and identify where an AI agent delivers the most value, and where human judgment needs to stay in the loop. Turn what you learn into a clear, prioritized view of what the agent should do next, backed by customer evidence rather than intuition. Build working prototypes to test agent workflows with real users before engineering commits to a full build. Translate customer needs into product requirements and an engineering-ready spec for your piece of the agent. Define the success metrics that tell us the agent is actually working: accuracy, time saved, trust, and adoption. Set the release criteria the agent must meet before it reaches customers, including how we evaluate reliability on real-world quoting scenarios. Analyze the value the agent creates for customers, how usage actually behaves, and what it costs us to serve. Study how other AI products charge for agentic work, from seats to usage to outcomes, and what those models would mean for our customers and our margins. Recommend how Parspec should charge for AI-powered work, with the analysis to back it up, and present it to leadership. . Skills. Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree (undergraduate or graduate) in Business, Economics, Engineering, Computer Science, Data Analytics, or a related field. Strong analytical skills, including comfort working through usage data, unit economics, and pricing trade-offs. Structured problem-solving ability: you can break an ambiguous question down into an answerable workplan. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to run a customer interview and write a spec an engineer can build from. A real ownership mentality: you have taken something from question to shipped result without being managed through it. Genuine interest in AI products: you have used them seriously, formed opinions on what works and what does not, and can articulate why. Prior professional experience in product management, consulting, software engineering, or a startup operating role. Hands-on experience building with or evaluating LLMs and AI agents, even on personal projects. Exposure to B2B SaaS business models, pricing and packaging work, or product discovery methods. Interest in construction, building materials, industrial distribution, or vertical SaaS businesses. Experience in fast-paced or early-stage startup environments. . Benefits. Direct, sustained working exposure to Parspec's product leadership. Ownership of a live piece of an AI product that real customers use, with a spec and a pricing recommendation the company acts on. Hands-on experience with the two defining challenges of AI product management: making agents reliable and making them commercially viable. A strong foundation and a credible story for future roles in AI product management, product leadership, or founding. Flexible hybrid work environment. . Company Overview.
