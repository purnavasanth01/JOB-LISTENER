Summary of Role: Launch your engineering career through hands-on hardware/software integration and validation of next-generation RISC-V CPUs and AI accelerators. Essential Responsibilities include: Execute compliance tests, regressions, benchmarks, and AI workloads across simulation, emulation, FPGA, and hardware platforms. Reproduce, debug, document, and track failures using logs, traces, waveforms, and performance counters. Develop automation for test execution, result checking, reporting, and dashboards. Collaborate with architecture, RTL, verification, compiler, software, performance, and infrastructure teams to resolve issues. Document test procedures and findings, and communicate progress clearly across globally distributed teams.
