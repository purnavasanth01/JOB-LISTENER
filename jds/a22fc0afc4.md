## Job description (verbatim)

Lindt & Sprüngli USA is hiring a Digital Commerce Retail Media Analytics Intern to build a scalable dashboard connecting retail media and commerce performance data. The intern will analyze campaign results, identify performance drivers, and develop actionable recommendations to support media investment decisions.. . Responsibilities. Build a Power BI retail media effectiveness dashboard that consolidates campaign, media, and commerce performance from multiple data sources into a clear, repeatable reporting solution. Collect, clean, transform, and validate data from Walmart Connect, Amazon Ads, Roundel, Kroger Precision Marketing, Instacart, DoorDash, agency reporting, and other relevant sources. Define and document consistent KPI calculations, data definitions, refresh processes, and quality checks to improve comparability and confidence across retail media networks. Analyze media performance using measures such as spend, impressions, clicks, click-through rate, cost per click, attributed sales, return on ad spend, conversion, and other available platform KPIs. Partner with the Digital Commerce Analyst to connect retail media results with sales, share, traffic, conversion, promotional, and retailer performance insights where data is available. Identify trends, performance drivers, outliers, and optimization opportunities, then translate findings into concise, actionable recommendations for the Retail Media Manager and business partners. Collaborate with Shopper Marketing, Digital Commerce, analytics, and agency partners to gather requirements, reconcile inputs, and ensure the dashboard supports business decision-making. Create user documentation and present the final dashboard, methodology, key insights, and recommended next steps to cross-functional stakeholders. . Skills. Academic, internship, or project experience working with large datasets, marketing analytics, business analytics, or data visualization. Experience cleaning, joining, and analyzing data from multiple sources to answer a business question or build a reporting solution. Experience developing dashboards or analytical models in Power BI; coursework and portfolio projects are acceptable. Required proficiency in Microsoft Power BI, including data modeling, Power Query, DAX, dashboard development, and data visualization. Strong data science and analytical foundation, with working knowledge of data preparation, exploratory analysis, statistical reasoning, and measurement concepts. Proficiency in Excel. Ability to translate complex analyses into clear insights, compelling visualizations, and practical business recommendations. Strong attention to detail and commitment to data quality, documentation, and reproducible processes. Strong project management, communication, and collaboration skills, with the ability to manage multiple data inputs and stakeholder needs. Curiosity about retail media networks, digital marketing, eCommerce, and omnichannel shopper behavior. Be a sophomore, junior, senior, or graduate student enrolled in an accredited university degree program. Experience with digital marketing, retail media, eCommerce, consumer packaged goods, or advertising measurement is preferred but not required. Experience with SQL and Python or R for data manipulation and analysis is strongly preferred. Preferably in Data Science, Analytics, Statistics, Computer Science, Information Systems, Marketing Analytics, Economics, or a related quantitative field.
