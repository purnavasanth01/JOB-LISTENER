. What Are We Looking For: . We are looking for a motivated UX/UI Design Intern to join the Smart Research Platform team and help design and build intuitive experiences for enterprise AI applications. The ideal candidate has a foundation in user-centered design and UI development, along with an interest in simplifying complex workflows involving AI, data, research, and human review. You should be curious, collaborative, comfortable receiving feedback, and interested in taking ideas from early concepts through design, prototyping, and working UI implementations. . . Why This Role Is Important to Us: . The Smart Research Platform uses generative AI to automate and streamline research-intensive enterprise workflows. Great UX is essential to making these advanced capabilities understandable, trustworthy, and easy to use. In this role, you will contribute to real product experiences while gaining hands-on exposure to UX design, AI interaction patterns, front-end development, and the full product development lifecycle. . .
