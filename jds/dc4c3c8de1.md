## Job description (verbatim)

Relay is a technology startup building smart, rugged radios and a connected cloud ecosystem to improve communication and productivity for frontline workers. The Software Engineering Intern will collaborate on Relay’s custom communication devices across Android application development, Linux OS systems, hardware interfaces, and embedded firmware.. . Responsibilities. **Android** — fix bugs and build new features spanning application development (Kotlin, Java) and OS-level programming, which may touch the Linux Kernel, Java-based frameworks, shell scripts, or C/C++/Rust daemons. **Linux OS** — work below the Android layer on operating system tasks and hardware interfaces. **Embedded systems** — develop new embedded products or microcontroller firmware that runs inside or alongside existing Relay devices. . Skills. Currently enrolled in a Computer Science or related technical field with a GPA of 3.5 or above. Strong proficiency in C, Java, or Kotlin. Or experience in embedded systems programming — e.g., NRF or ST-based products running Zephyr, ThreadX, or Linux. Or experience programming Linux at or below the POSIX API layer, typically in C/C++ (Rust experience is increasingly relevant as AOSP adopts it). Or solid experience with scripting languages such as Bash, Python, or similar. Comfort with desktop and server Linux distributions — our development environments and internal tooling are Linux-based. An analytical, methodical approach to problem-solving. Strong communication skills and a habit of documenting what you build. Ability to manage multiple workstreams and self-prioritize. Exposure to Agile development practices. Genuine curiosity and drive to learn. Android/AOSP systems programming experience is a plus but not required. Familiarity with IP networking concepts (HTTP, TCP/UDP sockets) is preferred. . Benefits. Executive and senior leadership team exposure, including regular touchpoints with Relay's leadership team, Q&A coffee chats, Breakfast with the CEO, and working sessions.. Cross-functional cohort working alongside interns across every part of the business.. Structured learning through regular skill-building sessions covering product strategy, financial modeling, sales craft, professional networking, and interviewing skills.. Real ownership of a project from kickoff to final presentation, with mentors and fellow Relayers invested in your success.. Opportunity to present your work to the full company at the end of the summer.. Team lunches, a volunteer Intern Impact day, and social events at the Raleigh HQ..
