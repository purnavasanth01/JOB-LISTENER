L3Harris Missile Solutions Segment Business Intelligence team is seeking a Data Architecture Intern to support data-driven decision-making for missile systems development and operations. Working under the guidance of data architects, business intelligence analysts, and cross-functional partners, the intern will assist with data modeling, integration, reporting, and documentation activities across enterprise data platforms. This role provides hands-on exposure to data warehouse practices, data quality, data security, and scalable data solutions while contributing to meaningful team projects. . Essential Functions . Support the development, design, and maintenance of data warehouses and transformation tools that enable business insights through dashboards and reports. . Assist with data pipeline design and maintenance, including transformation mapping, data flow diagrams, and metadata collection. . Help document conceptual, logical, and physical data models, schemas, and metadata solutions. . Support the development and documentation of data ontologies, business entities, properties, and relationships. . Participate in planning sessions with customers and project teams to understand business needs and translate them into data integration and reporting requirements. . Contribute to extracting, transforming, analyzing, and visualizing data from platforms such as Snowflake and Palantir Foundry. . Support testing, troubleshooting, and data quality activities under the guidance of experienced team members. . Qualifications .
