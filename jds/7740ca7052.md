Job Description: FormFactor is seeking an AI/ML Engineering Intern to develop a working proof of concept decision support tool for the manufacturing review process. The intern will apply AI/ML methods to documented engineering rules and historical review records to help engineers find relevant prior cases, evaluate potential design concerns, and prepare more consistent and traceable review submissions. The initial use case will focus on design for manufacturability (DFM) violations. The primary deliverable will be a working prototype that demonstrates how engineering review data, design rules, and historical decisions can improve consistency, efficiency, and traceability within the manufacturing review process. The prototype will support engineering review and preparation; final technical decisions and approvals will remain with authorized reviewers. This internship provides hands on experience applying AI/ML techniques to real engineering workflows, partnering with design and manufacturing experts, and delivering a prototype with measurable business impact. Key Responsibilities: Clean, organize, and analyze historical manufacturing review records and outcome categories. Work with engineering stakeholders to define common DFM violation types and review outcomes. Develop methods to convert free text manufacturing issues and responses into structured information. Build a capability to identify applicable engineering rules and risk guidance. Develop search and ranking methods to identify relevant historical cases. Create an explainable assessment that shows the applicable rule, supporting historical cases, potential risk areas, and uncertainty. Compare rule based, historical case based, and combined AI/ML approaches. Evaluate the prototype using historical test cases and feedback from engineering experts. Document the solution architecture, assumptions, limitations, data quality considerations, and opportunities for future expansion. Present the working prototype, evaluation results, and recommended next steps to engineering stakeholders. Preferred Qualifications & Experience: Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Machine Learning, or a related field. Programming experience in Python and experience working with tabular data and text. Understanding of machine learning fundamentals and model evaluation. Ability to organize ambiguous technical information and communicate findings clearly. Ability to work with proprietary engineering data in a controlled internal environment.
