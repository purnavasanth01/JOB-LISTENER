OXOS Medical is hiring a UX/UI Design Intern to support user-centered development of its medical device software and cloud-based platform. The role focuses on mapping user journeys, researching and prototyping interface improvements, conducting usability testing, and maintaining human factors documentation and user materials.. . Responsibilities. Map MC2 and OXOS Platform user journeys and develop workflow models for new and future customers and use cases. Perform comprehensive user interface design research, including competitive analysis, use environment research, and best practice review. Collaborate with product and software development teams to design and prototype frontend UX/UI solutions, adhering to usability heuristics and design guidelines. Conduct design assessments and usability testing with internal stakeholders, customers, and users. Maintain and update existing Usability and Human Factors documentation, such as Task Analysis, Use Specifications, and User Interface Specifications. Improve and update user manuals and training guides for both MC2 and OXOS Platform to benefit new and existing customers. Contribute to the continuous development of the OXOS design guidelines and Figma component library. Explore and implement AI-enabled processes for modification of Figma designs and translation to product source code. . Skills. Strong experience with Figma design suite, including prototyping and reusable component use for design development and translation to usable software frontend designs. Experience in user journey mapping and wireframe prototyping for early development and design review with technical teams. Previous UX/UI Design or software product development experience. A portfolio of past design experience will be requested. Demonstrated ability to articulate and defend design rationale clearly and effectively to both technical (software developers) and non-technical (clinical, marketing) stakeholders.
