U.S. Venture is hiring a Cloud Engineer Intern to support day-to-day cloud operations, governance, and service support across Azure, GCP, and on-premises infrastructure. The intern will assist with service tickets, resource and access audits, platform operations, and operational documentation.. . Responsibilities. Respond to and triage service tickets submitted to the Cloud Platform & Enablement team, routing requests appropriately and escalating issues to senior engineers as needed. Assist with cloud resource inventory audits across Azure and GCP environments, identifying unused, misconfigured, or untagged resources and documenting findings for remediation. Support user access control reviews by auditing IAM roles, group memberships, and permissions across cloud platforms to ensure least-privilege principles are maintained. Perform cloud environment clean-up tasks including decommissioning orphaned resources, enforcing tagging standards, and assisting with cost hygiene activities. Assist in executing routine platform operations such as monitoring checks, patch validation, and backup verification following documented runbooks. Document procedures, audit findings, and ticket resolutions to support team knowledge sharing and operational readiness. Participate in team meetings, stand-ups, and collaborative working sessions to understand ongoing projects and contribute to team goals. Cloud Resource Inventory Audit & Clean-Up: Conduct a comprehensive inventory audit across Azure and GCP subscriptions/projects, identify unused or non-compliant resources, and assist in executing a clean-up plan in coordination with the team. User Access Controls Review: Partner with senior engineers to perform a review of IAM roles and permissions across cloud platforms, producing a summary report of findings and recommended remediations. Service Ticket Analysis & Documentation: Analyze incoming service ticket patterns, help develop or improve runbook documentation for recurring request types, and assist in building a self-service knowledge base article for common issues. .
