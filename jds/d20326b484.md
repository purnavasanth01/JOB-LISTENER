## Job description (verbatim)

Vervint is a purpose-driven global consultancy that helps people and organizations thrive through technology, strategy, and human-centered experience design. The User Experience Design Intern will support research, design, client-facing sessions, project delivery, and the creation of user experience and service design deliverables.. . Responsibilities. Deliver exceptional service to our internal and external clients. Support execution of internal and external projects from conception through delivery. Help conduct user research to uncover key insights, inform innovation, and inspire design. Participate in the design and facilitation of discovery, alignment or innovation sessions and other client-facing meetings. Contribute to overall project quality and success by wholly owning or assisting with the production of project deliverables (i.e. product roadmaps, user personas, service blueprints, user flows, user stories, journey maps, alignment diagrams, sketches, wireframes, prototypes, visual concepts, strategic communication). Build and maintain close relationships with our partners in development, QA, client services as well as other design team members to ensure that all requirements and design decisions are clearly understood and delivered. Develop an understanding of consulting practices including client services, budgeting, timelines, and processes. . Skills. Minimum two years of study or experience in experience design, qualitative research, service design, web design, graphic design, industrial design, human factors design, or other related disciplines. Ability to synthesize information and communicate difficult and unfamiliar concepts in plain language. Sketching, wireframing, and prototyping skills (Figma, InDesign, Zeplin, Miro and Marvin are our core tools). User research, journey mapping, service blueprinting, persona, or jobs to be done experience. Familiarity with human-centered design and design thinking. Willingness and drive to learn new skills (including any of the above you may not have today) and adopt new ways of working. Comfort with some level of ambiguity. . Benefits. Hybrid work arrangement. . Company Overview. Vervint is a technology consulting firm that provides software quality assurance, mobile development, and disaster recovery services. It was founded in 1997, and is headquartered in Grand Rapids, Michigan, USA, with a workforce of 201-500 employees. Its website is http://www.vervint.com.. .
