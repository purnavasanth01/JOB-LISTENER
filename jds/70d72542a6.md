## Job description (verbatim)

Note: The job is a remote job and is open to candidates in USA. Blueprint builds AI support tools for therapists. The Product Engineering Intern will own and ship product features across the backend and frontend, contribute to AI-powered clinical workflows, and participate in the engineering team's development practices.. . Responsibilities. Own a bounded project end to end, from scoping to shipping, with a dedicated mentor. Ship AI-powered features across our NestJS backend and React frontend to production, used by tens of thousands of clinicians. Contribute to our LLM-powered features: progress-note generation, the clinical Assistant, insurance automation, and the evals that keep them safe and accurate. Write tests, go through code review, and take part in standups and planning like any other engineer on the team. See up close how a product team evaluates, adopts, or rejects new AI tools and techniques, in both the product and the way we build it. . Skills. You can read, write, and reason thoughtfully about code, and you're comfortable getting dropped into an unfamiliar codebase. You're product-minded: you want the 'why' behind the work, not just the spec, you weigh tradeoffs, and you care whether what you shipped helped our clinician and their clients. You see AI as a tool for solving real problems, not an end in itself: you reach for it in your own work (or you're eager to), and you're thoughtful about where it helps and where it doesn't. You're curious about AI and LLMs: how to prompt, evaluate, and apply them responsibly where the stakes are real. You communicate clearly in writing, which matters on a remote team, and you actively welcome feedback. You are highly self-motivated and and are comfortable working independently (with mentorship). Familiarity with Typescript, React, Node/NestJS, or Postgres. Exposure to LLM work (prompting, RAG, agents, evals). Experience/interest in the healthcare and mental health space. . Benefits. 100% remote. Hardworking, mission-driven, friendly coworkers. Flexibility with scheduling; they'd love you full-time and are willing to work with you to find the right schedule, with at least 20+ hours a week. A dedicated mentor. . Company Overview.
