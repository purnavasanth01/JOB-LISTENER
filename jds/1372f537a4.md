## Job description (verbatim)

. About the COO organization . The Chief Operating Office organization supports the execution, technology, data, operating platforms and transformation capabilities that enable State Street Investment Management to serve its clients and deliver against its strategic priorities.. As a summer intern within Fixed Income Technology, you will work with experienced professionals on meaningful business initiatives while gaining exposure to how a global investment-management organization operates, transforms and collaborates across business and technology teams. This role provides an opportunity to work collaboratively with portfolio managers, operations, and technology teams to build resilient, scalable platforms for liquidity management, portfolio construction, repo, trade workflows, controls, and reporting.. Be part of the Fixed Income technology organization supporting and modernizing Cash Investment Management, building scalable engineering capabilities that improve investment workflows, data and decision-making. Help address real business and technology challenges across liquidity and cash management, portfolio construction, trading, compliance, reconciliation, controls and reporting, with a focus on automation and modernization. Support an engineering initiative focused on applying data and automation to a selected Fixed Income investment workflow, from understanding the problem through developing a working solution or prototype. Gain exposure to the end-to-end investment ecosystem, including portfolio managers, compliance and operations, while working alongside engineering, architecture and technology teams.. . What you will do . As a COO summer intern, you will:. Support the design, development, and testing of a defined engineering solution addressing a Fixed Income Cash Investment Management workflow, such as cash visibility, portfolio construction, trading, controls, or reconciliation.. Analyze business processes, data flows, and existing technology workflows to identify opportunities for automation, simplification, improved data quality, and scalability.. Collaborate with engineering teams and business partners across portfolio management, trading, credit, risk, compliance, and operations to understand requirements and translate them into technology solutions.. Gain hands-on experience with modern software engineering, APIs, cloud technologies, distributed systems, and data engineering while contributing to the implementation and documentation of the solution.. Document and present the completed solution at the end of the internship, including the business problem, design approach, implementation, results, and recommendations for future development.. Participate in program learning, networking and professional-development activities.. What you will learn . During the internship, you will have opportunities to develop:.
