The City of New York’s Administration for Children’s Services (ACS) seeks an Information Technology and Data Analysis Fellow for the Division of Child Protection. The role supports child protective practice by analyzing agency data, monitoring performance, creating reports and visualizations, and preparing data-driven materials for borough leadership.. . Responsibilities. Develop proficiency in ACS data systems and technology platforms used to support child protective practice. Pull, compile, organize, and analyze statistical data from ACS systems and other authorized data sources. Monitor key child protective investigation indicators, including timeliness, case activity, safety assessments, supervisory review, service referrals, and case outcomes. Compare data across units, teams, time periods, and identified performance indicators to assist leadership in understanding areas of strength and opportunities for improvement. Assist with developing spreadsheets, dashboards, charts, and other data summaries for Borough leadership. Use Review Tools to examine case documentation and case activity to identify patterns related to assessment, safety planning, family engagement, supervisory oversight, and case progression. Develop recurring data reports and informational summaries to support Borough leadership meetings and performance discussions. Assist the Borough Commissioner in preparing data-driven presentations and briefing materials. Track progress toward identified Borough goals and initiatives.
