## Job description (verbatim)

ABOUT STRAVA Strava is the app for active people. With over 200 million athletes in more than 185 countries, it’s more than tracking workouts—it’s where people make progress together, from new habits to new personal bests. No matter your sport or how you track it, Strava’s got you covered. Find your crew, crush your goals, and make every effort count. Start your journey https://www.strava.com/subscription with Strava today. Our mission is simple: to motivate people to live their best active lives. We believe in the power of movement to connect and drive people forward. As an Engineering Intern at Strava, you will be embedded in a team working on real projects that ship to our athletes and within the company, treated like a full-time member of the engineering team. You'll collaborate with cross functional teams that help us support and deliver to our athletes faster, while keeping them inspired and motivated. We’ll give you experienced mentors and all the tools you need to make a meaningful impact on our product. To see examples of what past interns have contributed, read our Engineering Blog https://medium.com/strava-engineering/all?topic=internships! Available Internship: Strava is looking for software engineering interns for a range of disciplines. This internship is on the Subscriptions team, which owns how athletes discover, start, and manage a Strava subscription. That covers paywalls, checkout, and billing through Google Play and the App Store. The work is central to Strava’s growth and reaches millions of athletes around the world. As an Android intern, you’ll spend most of your time in Kotlin and Compose, building the paywalls and purchase flows athletes see every day. On Subscriptions, platform boundaries are a starting point, not a wall: our engineers regularly ship across Android, iOS, and the services behind them, and you’ll be invited to do the same. You’ll work alongside engineers on every platform, product managers, and designers, and learn from Strava’s Android Guild, our community of Android engineers across the company. We follow a flexible hybrid model that requires you in our SF office four days per week. What You’ll Do: - Ship features on the Subscriptions roadmap, learning how we take an idea from concept to production on a team that releases weekly - Build subscription experiences like paywalls and purchase flows, working hands-on with Google Play and App Store billing - Run experiments across the subscription lifecycle and learn how we measure their impact - Prototype with AI coding tools like Claude Code, and use visual mockups to collaborate with design and product - Learn from experienced engineers how we make tradeoffs, build maintainable systems, and test features to a high quality bar - Follow your features across the stack, starting on Android and going into Go, Scala, or Swift when the work takes you there, paired with teammates who know that code We’re excited about you because: - You have hands-on experience building Android apps in Kotlin, whether through coursework, internships, or personal projects - You’re eager to build features end to end and put polished product design in athletes’ hands - You’re curious about how the whole product works, not just your platform. No iOS or backend experience needed; we’ll build those skills together. - You have a builder’s mindset: when the right tool doesn’t exist yet, you’re energized to create it - Experience with Jetpack Compose, Coroutines, or GraphQL is a plus - Are currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, with an expected graduation date of May 2027, December 2027, May 2028 — OR – have recently completed or are currently enrolled in a coding bootcamp, technical training program, or are transitioning into software engineering through equivalent practical experience. The Summer Internship Program: - The program will be in our San Francisco, CA office with a hybrid office attendance requirement of 4 days per week - Housing Stipend for Summer is provided to live in SF for 3 months - Length of program: 10-12 weeks (Summer 2027 roughly June through August). - Working Hours: 40 hours per week - This is a paid internship.
