R&D Intern, Golden Path Automation . Keysight Technologies is seeking an R&D Intern to support the development of Golden Path automation, a key initiative aimed at making software delivery faster, simpler, and more consistent across the organization. A Golden Path is a documented, automated, and supported delivery workflow that embeds approved development, security, compliance, and operational practices, so teams can follow a paved road instead of building their own CI/CD pipelines and compliance processes.. This internship offers a unique opportunity for students to gain hands-on experience in DevOps, automation, and product security while contributing to an impactful, high-visibility project within Keysight’s technology ecosystem.. Key Responsibilities . Define, document, and build the automation for a C++ Conan library Golden Path. . Create workflows, templates, and self-service capabilities within Keysight’s Internal Developer Platform. . Embed security scanning and compliance checks into delivery workflows so developers meet requirements by default. . Collaborate with engineering, security, and IT teams to validate the Golden Path with real software projects. . Test and document Golden Path components so teams can adopt them with minimal effort. .
