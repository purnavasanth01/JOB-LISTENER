FreshDirect is seeking a Product Intern to support its product management team in developing digital products that shape the customer shopping journey. Cross-functional Collaboration: Work closely with relevant stakeholder teams such as, design, merchandising, marketing, and operations to help define requirements, participate in product discussions, and ensure smooth execution. Roadmap Assistance: Contribute to maintaining product roadmaps, documenting requirements, updating timelines, and tracking progress across development cycles.
