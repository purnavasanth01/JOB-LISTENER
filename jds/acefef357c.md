## Job description (verbatim)

Position Summary. The SSDI Product Development team develops and launches data products that combine State Street proprietary data, third-party data, quantitative research, and modern analytics to address institutional-investor needs. The Summer Intern will work with product managers, researchers, data engineers, technology teams, and business partners to support the full product lifecycle, from market discovery and concept development through prototyping, documentation, launch preparation, and performance monitoring.. This role is designed for a curious, analytical student who wants hands-on exposure to financial data products, private and public markets, product strategy, and AI-enabled workflows. The intern will own a defined summer project and present findings and recommendations to the team at the end of the program.. . Key Responsibilities. · Market and client research: Analyze industry trends, client needs, competing data platforms, and emerging technologies; synthesize findings into concise product recommendations.. · Product design and requirements: Help translate business problems into product concepts, user stories, functional requirements, data specifications, and acceptance criteria.. · Data analysis and prototyping: Use Excel, Python, SQL, Databricks, or similar tools to explore datasets, assess coverage and quality, create prototypes, and communicate insights through clear visualizations.. · Data quality and validation: Perform reasonableness checks, investigate anomalies, document data lineage and assumptions, and critically review analytical and AI-generated outputs.. · Product implementation: Coordinate with research, data engineering, technology, operations, legal, and commercial teams to track deliverables, risks, dependencies, decisions, and action items.. · Product documentation and commercialization: Support product briefs, methodology notes, presentations, user guides, competitive comparisons, and internal or client-facing materials.. · AI-enabled productivity: Apply approved generative AI tools, including Microsoft Copilot, to research, drafting, summarization, meeting follow-ups, and workflow improvement while maintaining accuracy, confidentiality, and appropriate human review.. · Summer project: Complete a scoped product-development assignment with measurable outputs, such as a market landscape, data feasibility assessment, prototype dashboard, product requirements document, or go-to-market recommendation.. Learning Opportunities. · Gain practical experience across the data-product lifecycle in a global financial-services organization.. · Learn how institutional investors use data, benchmarks, indicators, and analytics across public and private markets.. · Develop skills in product management, quantitative analysis, stakeholder communication, and executive-ready storytelling.. · Build a portfolio-quality final project and present it to SSDI leaders and cross-functional partners.. . Qualifications. · Currently pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in finance, economics, business, data science, computer science, statistics, mathematics, engineering, or a related field.. · Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with comfort working with structured data and drawing defensible conclusions.. · Working knowledge of Excel and PowerPoint; exposure to Python, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, or other analytical tools is preferred.. · Interest in financial markets, investment data, product development, quantitative research, or financial technology.. · Clear written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical audiences.. · Strong attention to detail, intellectual curiosity, sound judgment, and the ability to manage multiple tasks and deadlines.. · Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively in a cross-functional, fast-paced environment.. · Interest in generative AI and prompt design, with a commitment to validating outputs and following enterprise data-handling requirements.. . What Success Looks Like. · Delivers a well-structured summer project that addresses a real product or client need and includes clear evidence, assumptions, and recommended next steps.. · Produces accurate, reusable analysis and documentation that the team can carry forward after the internship.. · Communicates progress proactively, incorporates feedback, and builds effective working relationships across business and technical teams.. .
