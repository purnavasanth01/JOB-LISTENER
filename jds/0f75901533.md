WHAT YOU WILL DO - As a Video Generation Research Intern, you will work closely with our research and engineering teams to advance post-training techniques for large-scale video generation models, with a particular focus on controllability, generation quality, and temporal consistency. - You will explore cutting-edge generative modeling techniques and contribute to the development of next-generation AI-powered content creation and interactive virtual world experiences. You Will - Research, develop, and implement post-training methods for video generation models, including supervised fine-tuning, preference optimization, and reinforcement learning-based approaches. - Explore controllable video generation techniques to improve motion control, camera movement, trajectory following, and action-conditioned generation. - Design and conduct experiments to improve video generation quality, temporal consistency, motion realism, and alignment with user instructions. - Collaborate with researchers and engineers to develop training pipelines, evaluation benchmarks, and scalable experimentation workflows. - Stay up to date with the latest advances in video generation and generative AI, and contribute to research publications and practical applications. WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR - Currently pursuing a Master's or PhD degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Machine Learning, or a related technical field. - Strong research background and hands-on experience in video generation, including training or fine-tuning video generative models. - Solid understanding of modern generative modeling techniques, such as diffusion models, flow matching, Diffusion Transformers (DiTs), and autoregressive models. - Strong proficiency in Python and deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch. - Ability to independently implement research ideas, conduct experiments, analyze results, and collaborate effectively with a research team. PLUSES - Publications at top-tier AI and computer vision conferences, such as CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, ICML, ICLR, or NeurIPS. First-author publications related to video generation, especially controllable video generation, are strongly preferred. - Research experience in controllable video generation, including camera control, motion control, trajectory-guided generation, action-conditioned generation, or interactive video generation. - Experience with large-scale video model training, distributed training, or training and inference optimization. - Contributions to influential open-source video generation projects or research implementations. We are an equal opportunity employer that believes diverse backgrounds are key to bringing our concepts to life.
