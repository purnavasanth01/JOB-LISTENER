## Job description (verbatim)

Note: The job is a remote job and is open to candidates in USA. Businessolver is seeking a Product Design Intern to support the design of mobile experiences that help members understand and manage their benefits. The intern will contribute to research, user flows, wireframes, prototypes, usability testing, visual design, and documentation while collaborating with cross-functional partners.. . Responsibilities. Learn our members, products, benefits domain, and mobile experience. Review existing mobile journeys and identify usability or comprehension opportunities. Support customer research, stakeholder interviews, competitive reviews, and usability testing. Create user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs under guidance. Explore mobile experiences for iOS and Android while learning relevant platform conventions. Help identify important states and edge cases, including loading, empty, error, authentication, and permissions states. Participate in design critiques and share work early for feedback. Collaborate with Product Managers and Engineers to understand user needs, business goals, and technical constraints. Support design documentation, reusable patterns, and design-system updates. Review implemented designs with the team and help identify opportunities to improve quality. Complete a defined mobile design project from problem understanding through recommendation, prototype, testing, or launch support. Present your work, decisions, learnings, and next steps to the team. . Skills. Currently pursuing a degree in interaction design, UX, product design, graphic design, human-computer interaction, psychology, computer science, or a related field—or equivalent experience. A portfolio or work sample showing design thinking. Academic, personal, volunteer, or freelance projects are welcome. Familiarity with Figma or a similar design tool. Interest in mobile app design and digital products. Basic understanding of user flows, wireframing, prototyping, or usability testing. Strong curiosity and willingness to learn. Ability to communicate ideas clearly and receive feedback constructively. Good attention to detail and an interest in making complex experiences easier to understand. Ability to work collaboratively and manage agreed-upon deadlines. Experience designing for iOS, Android, or mobile-first products. Coursework or project experience in user research, interaction design, accessibility, content design, or design systems. Experience designing complex workflows, forms, account experiences, or transactional products. Familiarity with accessibility and inclusive-design principles. Interest in healthcare, insurance, employee benefits, or other products where clarity and trust matter.
