CliquePrize is hiring a Data Analytics Intern to map ZIP codes to metro markets, analyze geospatial, demographic, and business data, and build visualizations and KPI reports. The intern will also use AI tools to create presentations and deliverables for business planning and university academic credit.. . Responsibilities. Built an automated workflow to assign 800+ ZIP codes to their nearest Metro Market across all U.S. states using Python (GeoPandas, Numpy, Pandas), SQL (Dbeaver), and AI tools like Microsoft Copilot and Julius.ai. Retrieved ZIP code and centroid datasets from public sources ( U.S. Census Bureau, OpenDataSoft, World Population Review ) and standardized them for analysis. Defined state-specific assignment rules and validated proximity calculations using dynamic distance thresholds tailored to each state's geography and Metro Market configuration. Documented methodology, logic, and outputs using Claude.ai for reproducibility and future operational use. Created interactive geospatial maps in Looker Studio and Power BI to visualize Metro Markets and assigned ZIP codes for all launched and upcoming states. Identified misalignments in ZIP-to-market assignments by analyzing map layers, validating spatial relationships, and using Google Earth Pro for additional overlay checks. Exported finalized maps as images or PDFs to support presentation decks and strategic planning discussions. Analyzed affluent ZIP codes by Metro Market and mapped the distribution of Core Category Small Businesses relative to these high-value areas.
