Boehringer Ingelheim is seeking a Data Product Analyst Intern to join its Data Strategy Office and support data-driven business solutions. Assist with intake, documentation, prioritization, and tracking of data product requests and enhancement opportunities. Support backlog management, sprint planning, release tracking, and agile delivery activities. Conduct data analysis and exploratory research to identify trends, uncover insights, and support product decisions. Help maintain product documentation, Confluence pages, JIRA work items, dashboards, scorecards, and roadmaps. Assist in user acceptance testing (UAT), data validation, and quality assurance activities. Monitor data product usage, adoption, and performance metrics, providing recommendations for continuous improvement. Document business processes, best practices, data definitions, workflows, and user feedback. Support the creation of dashboards, reports, and product performance scorecards that communicate business value and usage trends. Participate in stakeholder interviews, workshops, and product discovery sessions to better understand user needs and business objectives. Assist with rapid prototyping and proof-of-concept activities to evaluate new data, analytics, and AI-enabled solutions. Act as a trusted partner to user communities by helping identify pain points, gathering feedback, and recommending solutions that drive adoption and business impact. Develop a product management mindset by helping define success metrics, evaluate product performance, prioritize opportunities, and improve user experience.
