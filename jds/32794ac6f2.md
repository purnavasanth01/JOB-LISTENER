Idaho National Laboratory is hiring a Stochastic Tools Software Intern to develop and improve capabilities in the MOOSE Stochastic Tools Module. The intern will build, test, document, and present scientific software supporting robust and scalable uncertainty quantification workflows.. . Responsibilities. Design, implement, and test a capability in the MOOSE Stochastic Tools Module in collaboration with framework developers. Improve the reliability of sensitivity analysis methods across a wide range of models and sampling strategies. Improve how stochastic studies use parallel resources when distributing simulations across processors. Make long-running stochastic studies more resilient, so they tolerate individual simulation failures, recover progress, and make results available while they run. Follow MOOSE software practices, including version control and issue tracking with GitHub, code review, and automated regression testing. Document your work and present results to technical staff and program leadership. Where appropriate, contribute to a technical report, conference paper, or journal publication. . Skills.
