The Networks Operations TAC SME team supports the operational reliability and performance of Samsung’s 4G and 5G wireless network solutions. The team collaborates with all operational support levels including the customer facing Technical Assistance Center (TAC), Engineering, R&D, and other customer-facing organizations to investigate complex network issues, identify recurring failure patterns, improve troubleshooting processes, and reduce service-restoration time.. The intern will help develop data-driven and AI-assisted solutions to improve operational efficiency and customer self-sufficiency. The primary assignment will involve analyzing historical incident and ticket data to identify recurring issues, failure trends, contributing factors, and potential predictive indicators. The intern will also evaluate related technical content—including product documentation, MOPs, troubleshooting guides, knowledge articles, alarms, and system logs - recommend efficient troubleshooting steps and surface relevant technical guidance based on reported symptoms and network context.. Additional assignments may include automating repetitive analysis, developing operational dashboards, improving knowledge organization and retrieval, identifying documentation gaps, and creating self-service guidance for common customer issues. The intern will work with experienced telecom engineers to define requirements, validate technical accuracy, measure the solution’s effectiveness, document the design, and present findings and recommendations at the conclusion of the internship. .
