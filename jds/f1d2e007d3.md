## Job description (verbatim)

Note: The job is a remote job and is open to candidates in USA. Testify Labs is offering an unpaid, remote training internship in QA automation. The intern will practice writing and reviewing Playwright tests, running tests in CI, and reporting bugs, with mentor guidance and a capstone test suite.. . Responsibilities. Writing Playwright end-to-end tests in TypeScript: locators, fixtures, assertions, retries and tracing. Designing test cases: what to test, how to name and tag tests, and how to keep a suite maintainable. Reading AI-generated test suites critically: spotting flaky selectors, missed journeys and false passes. Running tests in CI with GitHub Actions, and reading reports (Playwright HTML, Allure). Writing bug reports that developers can act on: steps, expected and actual results, screenshots and traces. . Skills. Are studying computer science or a related field, are in a bootcamp, or are switching careers into QA or software engineering. Know some Python, JavaScript or TypeScript (school or personal projects count). Like breaking things and explaining exactly how they broke. Can commit 8–10 hours a week and communicate clearly in writing. Any experience with Playwright, Cypress or Selenium. Familiarity with Git and GitHub. Interest in LLMs and AI agents. . Benefits. A weekly 1:1 with a mentor, and code review on every pull request. A public portfolio of Playwright tests you wrote, in your own repo. A LinkedIn recommendation and a reference letter when you finish the program. Remote training internship. Flexible hours; set your own hours around school or work. . Company Overview.
