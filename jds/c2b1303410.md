## Job description (verbatim)

Paylocity is hiring a Test Engineer Intern for its Implementation Engineering team. The intern will support software quality and test engineering for internal tools, integrations, and automations by designing and executing tests, validating business workflows, documenting defects, and collaborating with engineers and end users.. . Responsibilities. Review requirements, user stories, process maps, acceptance criteria, prior defects, and end-user feedback; translate them into clear, risk-based test plans, scenarios, test cases, test data, and expected results. Perform API testing for REST-based services, including requests and responses, status codes, schemas, authentication and authorization, validation rules, error handling, data persistence, negative conditions, boundary conditions, and repeat-run behavior. Execute functional, smoke, regression, integration, exploratory, usability, and end-to-end testing across supported environments and workflows. Validate complete business journeys, including user actions, APIs, integrations, data updates, workflow decisions, approvals, downstream systems, notifications, and final user-visible outcomes. Test permissions, role-based access, restricted actions, intended task actors, notification recipients, and data isolation between users, companies, clients, modules, and environments. Test dependencies and failure modes, including unavailable resources, missing forms or files, invalid references, partial success, delays, timeouts, retries, safe skips, recovery behavior, and clear user-facing warnings. Use representative company and client configurations to verify that company-specific data, identifiers, attachments, security groups, notifications, and other sensitive references are not exposed or carried into the wrong destination. Create actionable Jira defects with reproducible steps, expected and actual results, environment and test-data details, screenshots or logs, severity, business impact, and links to related requirements or test cases. Partner with engineers, subject-matter experts, and end users to facilitate user acceptance testing, verify fixes, summarize pass/fail status and residual risks, and maintain reusable regression suites, checklists, evidence, and test documentation. . Skills. Active enrollment in a bachelor's degree program with an anticipated graduation between December 2027 and May 2028. Must have a GPA of 3.0 or higher. Excellent time management, analytical thinking, persistence, and problem-solving skills. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to document evidence precisely, reproduce issues, and explain quality risks without overstating certainty. High attention to detail and commitment to process adherence, data responsibility, confidentiality, and working safely in approved environments. Preference for Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Information Systems, Data Science, Mathematics, or related technical majors with an interest in software quality and test engineering. Coursework, projects, or practical exposure related to software engineering, software testing, web applications, APIs, databases, automation, data analysis, or troubleshooting preferred. Strong technical aptitude; familiarity with HTTP, REST APIs, JSON, SQL, browser developer tools, logs, Git, Jira, or an API client such as Postman, Bruno, or Insomnia is helpful but not required. Exposure to a programming language such as Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, Java, or C# is preferred; willingness to learn is more important than mastery of a specific language. . Benefits.
