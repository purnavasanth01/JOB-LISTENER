Inside the Role Interns will gain knowledge and experience through exciting and real-life learning opportunities. Interns must work in accordance with DTNA’s core values of passion, respect, integrity and discipline. Internship positions can be located at various DTNA locations across the US. As a Cybersecurity Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) Intern, you will contribute to key risk management initiatives including information classification, policy and standards compliance, and the development and tracking of cybersecurity Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and Key Risk Indicators (KRIs). The role offers hands-on experience with both corporate Information Technology (IT) and plant floor Operational Technology (OT) environments, providing visibility into how cybersecurity frameworks and controls are applied across a hybrid enterprise. You will work alongside experienced practitioners to support risk management activities aligned to industry standards such as the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework (CSF), National Institute of Standards and Technology Special Publication (NIST SP) 800-53, and the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) 27000 series. Interns in this role may have the opportunity to transition into a full-time position subject to manager evaluations and business availability.
