## Job description (verbatim)

Princess Cruises is seeking a Digital Design Intern to support creative development across marketing channels and media types. The intern will assist senior designers with production work, create designs and layouts, and contribute to projects from kickoff through completion while learning design practices and applications.. . Responsibilities. The Digital Design Intern will assist in developing creative for a variety of marketing channels as well as a variety of media types. They will work closely with other graphic designers as well as the team’s creative leadership to learn best practices, industry trends, as well as tips and tricks within the design applications. They will work as a production artist and assist senior designers in finalizing work, as well as getting projects they will get to own from start to finish. They will also participate in team meetings, and project kickoffs that include marketing employees from different channels. Work with senior designers and add support as a production designer. Build new designs including ads, banners, direct mailers. Design and layout email communications. Learn about Front-end Development. Work alongside copy writers to match visuals to the message. Design logos. Help with product branding. Participate in team standup meetings and brainstorms. . Skills. Current undergraduate or graduate student pursuing a degree in Graphic Design, UI/UX design. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Interest in branding, communications, creative agencies or marketing. Proficiency with Adobe InDesign. Proficiency with Adobe Photoshop. Proficiency with Adobe Illustrator. Working knowledge of Figma. Working knowledge of Canva. Strong organizational skills and attention to detail. Previous internship within an agency or in-house agency. Understanding of Printing design and pre-press. Experience video editing and animation. Familiarity with Adobe After Effects, Adobe Lightroom, Adobe Bridge, Adobe Premiere. . Benefits. Competitive hourly wage provided. Full-time, 40 hours per week, in-office internship lasting 10 weeks. Structured learning opportunities designed to strengthen workplace, career, and leadership skills. Guidance, feedback, and support from leaders and professionals throughout the experience. Networking with fellow interns, employees, and leaders across participating brands and functions. Location-based programming and activities with interns from across Carnival Corporation. Present internship work, outcomes, and key learnings as part of a location-based showcase at the conclusion of the program. Strong performance may lead to consideration for future full-time, rotational, or other Emerging Talent opportunities based on business needs and available positions; participation does not guarantee future employment. .
