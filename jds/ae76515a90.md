## Job description (verbatim)

The Fund for Public Health in New York City is hiring a Food Access Data Intern to support data management, quality assurance, analysis, and reporting for the Nest & Nourish program. The intern will help ensure program data are accurate and useful for monitoring performance and informing improvements in food access and maternal health.. . Responsibilities. Support the collection, entry, organization, cleaning, and maintenance of program and evaluation datasets. Assist with tracking program indicators related to participant enrollment, food security, produce benefit distribution and redemption, nutrition education, engagement, retention, and outcomes. Conduct routine data quality checks to identify missing, inconsistent, or inaccurate information and assist with resolving discrepancies. Compile, summarize, and analyze quantitative data from surveys, program records, and other sources. Support the development of data summaries, tables, charts, dashboards, and reports for program monitoring, grant reporting, and evaluation. Assist with organizing evaluation materials, documenting data management procedures, and maintaining accurate records. Support additional evaluation activities, including organizing participant feedback and assisting with the review of qualitative data, as needed. Maintain confidentiality and adhere to applicable data privacy, security, and research requirements. Perform other duties as assigned to support the program’s data management, evaluation, and reporting goals. Other duties as assigned to support the overall goals and mission of the department/ organization. . Skills. Candidate must have completed or be pursuing a master's degree in public health, epidemiology, nutrition, food systems, public policy, evaluation, or a related field. Two to three years of relevant work, research, or project experience. Strong attention to detail, organizational skills, and follow-through. Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel, Word, and PowerPoint. Strong analytical, communication, writing, and problem-solving skills. Ability to work independently and collaboratively, manage multiple tasks, and adapt to changing priorities. Experience with statistical software (e.g., SAS, SPSS, R, or Stata), data visualization tools, or database management preferred. Interest in food access, food security, maternal and community health, and/or public health program evaluation preferred. . Benefits. Hybrid role. Hands-on experience in public health program evaluation, food access, and maternal health. . Company Overview.
