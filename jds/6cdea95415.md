## Job description (verbatim)

Note: The job is a remote job and is open to candidates in USA. Trace3 is an IT solutions and consulting company whose Innovation team researches emerging technology vendors, trends, and use cases across the Enterprise IT landscape. The Innovation Research Intern scouts and vets startups, supports client research requests, and helps maintain research resources and prepare insights for clients and internal teams.. . Responsibilities. Under the general direction of the SVP of Innovation, and with day-to-day guidance from an Innovation Researcher, the Innovation Research Intern is responsible for scouting and vetting emerging startups and use cases, and for supporting lower-complexity client research requests, with the goal of understanding how they fit within the Enterprise IT landscape. The intern serves as a research extension of the team, allowing researchers and principals to concentrate on analysis and advisory work. Scout emerging vendors across Security, Data, AI, Developer Operations, and others using funding databases, analyst coverage, venture portfolios, and news sources. Conduct secondary research to analyze investments in startups to determine possible trends. Apply deductive and inductive reasoning to test the accuracy of data, and clearly separate verified facts from vendor marketing claims. Interpret data to bring out meaningful insights by correlating different data points, citing sources for every material statement. Maintain a pipeline of candidate companies with consistent fields, including category, product summary, funding stage, investors, and leadership. Support the quality check process by verifying figures, names, and citations, and by keeping vendor tracking and research repositories current. Observe technical reviews with the Research team and targeted companies, and prepare notes with takeaways and follow-up questions. Assist researchers with client research requests, such as compiling vendor longlists, locating alternatives to an incumbent product, and building competitive comparisons. Support the preparation for client workshops and internal thought leadership by assembling background research. Complete a capstone research project near the end of the internship and present it to the team. All client-facing content is reviewed and approved by a Researcher or Innovation Principal; the intern does not contact vendors or clients without approval.
