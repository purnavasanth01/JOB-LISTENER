We're looking for an enthusiastic and curious Software Development Intern to join our IT team and contribute to real, impactful work. You'll get hands-on experience working on our subscription platform, building integrations, and using AI-assisted development tools to design, code, and test faster, all while learning how large-scale applications come together in a collaborative engineering environment. This is a great opportunity to grow your technical skills alongside experienced developers while making meaningful contributions from day one. . . What You'll Do . Work on Our Subscription Platform — Contribute to our subscription platform and its related services and upstream systems, helping deliver features, fixes, and enhancements while learning how enterprise-scale systems fit together. . Develop with AI — Use AI coding assistants and agentic tools (such as GitHub Copilot and Claude) to write, refactor, test, and debug code. Learn to prompt effectively, validate AI output, and recognize when not to trust it. . Help Build & Test Integrations — Contribute to building and documenting integrations between the subscription platform, upstream systems, and third-party services, with guidance from senior engineers. . Learn & Apply Best Practices — Participate in code reviews, follow coding standards, and develop good habits around code quality, testing, and API design, including how to critically review and validate AI-generated code. . Collaborate Across Teams — Work alongside developers, product managers, and business analysts to understand how business requirements translate into technical solutions. . Troubleshoot & Investigate — Help investigate bugs and performance issues, using AI tools to speed up root-cause analysis while learning how to diagnose problems in real production systems. . Contribute to Documentation — Use AI tools to help draft and maintain technical documentation, reviewing it for accuracy to support the broader team. . Deliver an Intern Project — Own a well-scoped project over the 10 weeks, from planning through delivery, and present your results and learnings to the team at the end of the internship. . .
