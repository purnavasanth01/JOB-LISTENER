## Job description (verbatim)

Stanley Black & Decker is seeking a UX/UI Design Intern to help develop user experiences for physical products across its portfolio. The intern will support research, ideation, interaction design, prototyping, documentation, and design communication throughout product development.. . Responsibilities. Support UX/UI design activities for product interfaces, including human-machine interfaces (HMI), displays, and controls. Assist with user research, competitive analysis, and synthesis of findings to identify design opportunities and inform product decisions. Create wireframes, user flows, interface concepts, prototypes, and visual design explorations that communicate experience direction clearly and effectively. Help translate complex user, technical, and business requirements into clear, intuitive, and usable experiences. Participate in design reviews and communicate design rationale to designers, engineers, product managers, and other stakeholders. Iterate on concepts based on feedback, usability insights, technical constraints, and evolving product requirements. Support the creation and maintenance of design standards, specifications, and supporting documentation to enable consistency across product experiences. Collaborate with industrial design, engineering, product management, and other cross-functional teams throughout the development process. Help prepare presentations and communication materials used to explain design concepts, recommendations, and decisions. Contribute to broader design initiatives and special projects as opportunities arise. . Skills. Strong Communication: Communicates ideas, questions, progress, and challenges clearly with teammates and cross-functional partners. Initiative: Takes ownership of assigned work, moves tasks forward proactively, and knows when to seek clarification or additional direction. Coachability: Welcomes feedback, applies it thoughtfully, and demonstrates a genuine willingness to learn and improve. Problem-Solving Mindset: Looks beyond the surface of a design challenge, considers user needs and real-world constraints, and can explain the reasoning behind design decisions. Currently pursuing a degree in UX/UI Design, Interaction Design, Industrial Design, Human-Computer Interaction, Human Factors, or a related field. Coursework or project experience in user experience, interaction design, product design, interface design, or related disciplines. Familiarity with design and prototyping tools such as Figma, Adobe Creative Cloud, or comparable software. Ability to create and communicate basic wireframes, flows, prototypes, and interface concepts. Strong visual, verbal, and presentation communication skills. Ability to work effectively in a collaborative, cross-functional environment. A portfolio demonstrating design thinking, problem-solving, and process is preferred. Exposure to physical product interfaces, embedded systems, or HMI design through coursework, projects, or internships. Familiarity with usability testing, design systems, accessibility principles, or interaction prototyping.
