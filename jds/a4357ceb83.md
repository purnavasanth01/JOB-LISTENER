DwyerOmega stores product marketing and technical files in Product Central, a SharePoint-based digital asset library, with metadata including product series and file type. An existing automation mirrors those files to AWS S3, and public URLs are built from that and served through Akamai. Today, associating those files to product models in Akeneo as assets is manual, so coverage is inconsistent and links go stale as files are replaced.. . . This intern will build an automation that, given a product model in Akeneo, locates the matching files in Product Central and either creates the corresponding Akeneo assets or updates existing asset links. The business will provide Akeneo API credentials, read access to Product Central and the S3 bucket, and a weekly technical check-in with the Product Central developer.. . . Essential Job Functions . Document mapping rules between Product Central metadata (product series, file type) and the Akeneo asset model, including where naming and metadata conventions break down. . Create a working script that matches files and writes assets and link updates into Akeneo via API, validated against one product series. . Handoff documentation and a coverage report showing assets created and stale links repaired.. Trigger the automation directly from Akeneo on a product model, and extend to a second product series.. .
