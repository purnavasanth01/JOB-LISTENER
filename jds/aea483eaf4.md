CNH is a company focused on agriculture and construction solutions that advances sustainability, productivity, and customer efficiency. The Parts Product Management Intern will support product strategies, product data quality, pricing, marketing, purchasing, and go-to-market initiatives through cross-functional collaboration and data analysis. The role will also contribute to sales growth, product awareness, profitability, reporting, and business recommendations.. . Responsibilities. Support Parts Product Managers in the development and maintenance of product family strategies and initiatives. Gather, validate, and document technical product specifications, features, benefits, and sales attributes for core Parts products. Collaborate with Pricing Analysts to support pricing-related projects and ensure product information is aligned across systems and channels. Partner with Parts Marketing to enhance product positioning, messaging, and awareness initiatives for internal and external audiences. Work closely with Purchasing teams to understand product sourcing, inventory considerations, and business requirements. Assist in maintaining and improving product data quality across multiple systems and platforms. Conduct research and analysis to identify opportunities for product family growth, market expansion, and competitive positioning. Support the creation of product documentation, presentations, training materials, and sales support tools. Coordinate with Information Technology (IT) and Business Intelligence teams to support data-related projects and reporting needs. Engage with key brand and dealer contacts to gather feedback and better understand customer and market needs. Analyze product performance metrics, sales trends, and customer insights to support business recommendations. Assist with special projects that drive product awareness, sales growth, and margin improvement across the Parts business. Present project findings, recommendations, and business insights to Product Management and cross-functional stakeholders. . Skills.
