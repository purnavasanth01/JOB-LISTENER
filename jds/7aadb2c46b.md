This internship provides an opportunity to apply classroom knowledge to real-world business challenges while gaining experience with data, analytics, and emerging technologies. Key Responsibilities Follow and promote Martin Marietta's safety, environmental, and ethical standards in all work activities. Actively participate in company initiatives and support a culture of operational excellence and continuous improvement. Conduct data exploration, extraction, cleansing, standardization, and wrangling to prepare data for analysis and modeling. Analyze data to identify trends, correlations, patterns, and opportunities that support business decision-making. Support the development, testing, and implementation of data science and machine learning solutions. Use AI-assisted development tools (GitHub Copilot, Snowflake Cortex Code) and standard engineering practices (Git version control, code reviews, CI/CD pipelines) to build, test, and document Python applications and APIs deployed on AWS and Snowflake. Assist in designing, building, and testing AI agents on Snowflake Cortex (Cortex Search, Cortex Analyst, Cortex Agents), including semantic models, document ingestion, and evaluation of agent responses. Support existing ML and optimization solutions through data validation, monitoring, and enhancements. Assist with building and maintaining data pipelines that load and transform data in Snowflake to support analytics, ML, and AI agent use cases. Collaborate with members of the Data Engineering & Science team and cross-functional business partners to understand business needs and develop data-driven solutions. Document analytical processes, methodologies, and solutions to support ongoing maintenance and knowledge sharing. Demonstrate initiative, professionalism, adaptability, and a strong work ethic while working independently and as part of a team. Perform all responsibilities in accordance with the Company's Mission, Vision, Values, policies, procedures, and Code of Ethical Business Conduct. Qualifications Currently pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in data science, mathematics, statistics, computer science, engineering, or another closely related quantitative field. Rising Junior, Senior, or Master's student preferred. Proficiency in Python and data science libraries. Experience developing in Python both in notebooks and in scripts or applications; familiarity with Git and SQL. Experience with one or more business intelligence or data visualization tools, such as Streamlit, Alteryx, Power BI, or Tableau is a plus. Exposure to Snowflake and AWS preferred; exposure to LLM APIs, prompt engineering, RAG, or AI agent frameworks is a strong plus. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and critical-thinking skills. Strong verbal and written communication skills. Ability to work independently with minimal supervision and effectively manage multiple priorities. Ability to collaborate effectively within a team environment. Strong interest in applying data science, machine learning, and AI to solve real-world business challenges.
