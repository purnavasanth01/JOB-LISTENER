## Job description (verbatim)

Trinidad Benham is hiring a UI/UX Design Intern to support implementation of its new manufacturing ERP system. The intern will design and validate user interfaces, conduct user research, maintain design-system components, and collaborate with analysts, engineers, and stakeholders on delivery and improvements.. . Responsibilities. Create UI mockups and interactive prototypes in Figma for desktop and mobile ERP features. Partner with the Business Analyst team to translate business requirements into creative and highly usable design solutions. Apply UI design fundamentals, including layout, visual hierarchy, typography, color, and accessibility, to assigned features. Evaluate existing screens against industry standards and usability heuristics and recommend improvements. Consider development effort and business value when proposing design solutions. Speak directly with plant, office, and field users to understand their work, their needs, and their pain points with the legacy system. Plan and conduct usability testing on prototypes and completed features. Run UI demonstrations with users and stakeholders and capture their feedback. Document research findings and apply them to design decisions. Maintain and extend a modular design system that stays consistent across desktop and mobile platforms. Advocate for consistent use of established components and interaction patterns. Document component usage so Software Engineers and other designers can apply it without guesswork. Participate in design syncs and critiques with designers and Software Engineers to refine assigned designs. Contribute to requirements gathering and help write user stories for features being designed. Work with Software Engineers during development to answer design questions and resolve implementation questions. Contribute to the standards, practices, and culture of a growing design team. Review completed functionality to verify it was built as designed. Identify, document, and prioritize visual and interaction defects. Support User Acceptance Testing by clarifying intended behavior for business stakeholders. Monitor user feedback following implementation and recommend design refinements. Maintain knowledge of current design tools, patterns, and emerging technologies. Utilize AI-assisted design and prototyping tools responsibly to speed up and improve work product while maintaining accountability for design quality and business outcomes.
