## Job description (verbatim)

Vibrantz Minerals LLC is hiring a Business Intelligence Intern to support its BI team in building reports and models using data from multiple ERP and business systems. The intern will contribute across data sourcing, modeling, reporting, and analysis, working with data engineers, analysts, and business leaders to develop solutions that inform decisions.. . Responsibilities. Write and tune T-SQL queries, views, and stored procedures. Develop Power BI reports and DAX measures on top of Microsoft Fabric, and help improve existing semantic models. Maintain and troubleshoot existing SSIS packages, Fabric pipelines or notebooks. Help identify and plan enhancements or modernization. Learn how data is structured in our ERP systems (SAP, IFS, Ross, Dynamics, etc.) and help bring it together for cross-system reporting. Gather, clean, and validate data from multiple source systems, including checking data quality and helping reconcile master data across sources. Take small projects from request to delivery: clarify what the requester needs, build a first version, gather feedback, and refine. Help standardize GitHub-based version control across SQL, pipelines, and report assets, including repository structure and team workflows. Document data sources, report logic, and refresh processes so others can pick them up. Troubleshoot report, refresh, and data issues, and support users who run into them. Monitor capacity, usage, and refresh performance, and flag problems early. Walk business users through new reports and answer their questions. . Skills. Pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Analytics, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent self-directed learning and project work. Ability to write SQL queries and procedures independently to retrieve, join, analyze and transform data. Understanding of relational database design, including keys, relationships, and how transactional data is organized (orders, inventory, finance, and similar). Experience building reports or visualizations in Power BI, Tableau, or a comparable tool. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, including breaking down an unclear request or a data discrepancy and working it through to an answer. Strong attention to detail, with the discipline to double-check your own work. Genuine curiosity about how data and business processes connect, and a drive to dig into the “why” behind the numbers. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including explaining technical work to non-technical users. Experience working with ERP data or a large business system. Familiarity with SAP, IFS, Aptean Ross, or Microsoft Dynamics is especially valuable. Power BI skills including DAX and Power Query. Knowledge of Microsoft Fabric (Lakehouse, Warehouse, Dataflows, Pipelines, Notebooks). Experience with SSIS or another ETL/ELT tool, and orchestrating scheduled jobs. Exposure to data warehousing concepts and how data moves from source systems into reporting models. Working knowledge of GitHub. Python for data work, particularly pandas or PySpark.
