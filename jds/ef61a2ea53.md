Overview. The hourly rate for this position is $17.75 for Undergraduate II and is non-negotiable. . . This position is open until filled . . This Statement of Work covers the need for student services to provide technical and analytical support in the field of Computer Science. The purpose of this task order is to obtain assistance with software development life cycle support for NIWC Pacific Business Applications project.. . The NIWC Pacific Business Applications system provides comprehensive software development life cycle (SDLC) support for the Command’s application suite. These applications consist of internally and externally developed Government off-the-shelf (GOTS) and commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) software which support a range of corporate functions such as finance, contracts, physical and personnel security, plant property, facilities, document control, project management, billing tool, and workforce management.. Responsibilities. The student contractor shall provide analytical and technical support in the following areas as specified under individual task orders, to the work sponsor.. . Testing & Quality Assurance Support:. Provide assistance with internal testing and User Acceptance Testing (UAT) for various web and tablet applications.. Support the coordination and execution of internal testing events within the Business Application suite.. Assist in reviewing developer test plans and providing feedback to ensure comprehensive testing coverage.. Support QA efforts by performing tests on developed products and documenting identified defects or potential enhancements.. Technical Documentation & Knowledge Management:. Assist in the development and sustainment of quick reference guides (QRGs) and comprehensive user manuals.. Support the updating of existing documentation to reflect software updates and process changes.. Provide support for the Project SharePoint / HUB website to ensure project resources and documentation are current and accessible.. Software Development Support:. Support the software promotion lifecycle by assisting in the deployment, configuration, and validation of application builds across the Development, Quality Assurance (QA), and Production environments.. Assist in the implementation of small-scale features, UI enhancements, and bug fixes.. Develop and execute unit tests to ensure the stability of individual software components and reduce regression errors.. Support the development of API endpoints and assist in the integration of front-end components with backend services.. Qualifications.
