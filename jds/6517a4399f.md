## Job description (verbatim)

About the Role . . Turn your classroom learning into real-world impact with H&R Block’s Embark 2027 Summer Internship Program, open to students studying Business Management/Administration , Product Management , Computer Science , and Business Analytics. This 11-week paid internship offers meaningful, real-world experience that aligns with your field of study while making a valuable impact on our organization. . . The Embark 2027 Summer Internship runs from May 17, 2027 – July 30, 2027. . . You’ll work at our corporate headquarters in the heart of downtown Kansas City’s Power & Light District, where you’re just steps, or a streetcar ride, away from incredible restaurants, entertainment, and city energy. Inside our building, you’ll have access to amenities that make work-life balance easy, including an onsite Starbucks, a company dining hall with plenty of options, a free fitness center, and free parking! . Program Highlights: . $25/hour compensation . Paid PTO during H&R Block’s annual Reboot Week (June 28 – July 2) . Housing stipend provided for students whose permanent address is more than 50 miles from the KC metro . . Throughout the 11-week internship, under the guidance of a leader, some of the internship responsibilities will include: . Support product discovery by researching customer needs and identifying opportunities that align with business goals. . Assist in writing clear user stories and acceptance criteria to guide engineering and design work. . Participate in product planning and ceremonies, helping manage the product backlog and prioritize features. . Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver features on time and with quality. . Use data and metrics to evaluate product performance and support data-driven decision making. . Engage in professional development to build product management, Agile, and collaboration skills. . Interns will be provided with significant guidance and mentoring but will also be encouraged to work independently as well. . . Throughout the 11-week internship, as a part of the Embark program, interns will also participate in workshops and events that align with the program's four pillars: . . Professional Development : includes the basics for a professional role, everything from using Microsoft Office to setting up a Teams meeting and giving a presentation. . . Giveback : involves participating in Make Every Block Better community service events. The goal is to teach the importance of community, make an impact, and provide bonding time for teams. . . Networking : entails meeting various executives with offices at H&R Block’s headquarters, having lunch with directors and vice presidents, spending time with a mentor and getting to know people beyond their assigned department. . . Connected Culture : is meant to be fun and teach the importance of working hard and playing hard. H&R Block might take the interns to a Kansas City Royals game or facilitate other competitions meant to be fun or instill teamwork. . . Required Qualifications . . Education: . High school diploma or equivalent . Seeking to obtain a degree in Business Administration, Business/Data Analytics, Computer Science, Business Management or some similar discipline with a GPA of 3.0 or above . Ability to work as part of a diverse team and independently . Highly motivated, organized, reliable, and detail-oriented . Strong written and verbal communication skills . Quick learner and not afraid to work with new computer-based programs and databases . Ability to be effective and contribute in a fast-paced corporate environment . Work Experience: . No previous work experience necessary. . . Preferred Qualifications . . Demonstrated interest in product management, digital products, customer experience, or product strategy . Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with an interest in using data and customer insights to inform product decisions . Ability to identify customer needs, define problems, and develop thoughtful solutions . Interest in customer research, user testing, and translating feedback into actionable product recommendations . Ability to collaborate effectively across Product, Design/UX, Engineering, and other cross-functional teams . Familiarity with or interest in Agile product development, roadmapping, product prioritization, and sprint-based ways of working . Comfort working with data, product metrics, dashboards, or analytics tools to identify trends and communicate insights . . Why work for us . .
