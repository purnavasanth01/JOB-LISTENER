## Job description (verbatim)

ABOUT GENMD GenMD is a Stanford spin-off building AI and data infrastructure for healthcare. We grew out of years of work inside Stanford Medicine, alongside world-class researchers and clinicians, and we now work with health systems and data partners on large longitudinal datasets: claims, health records, imaging and clinical notes. We make that data usable for researchers and frontier AI, while patient privacy stays protected. We're just coming out of stealth, already revenue-generating and well funded. We're intentionally small, move fast, and are focused on building something fundamental. Our CEO is a former Associate Director of AI at Stanford Medicine. See how we work: https://genmd.ai/careers ABOUT THE ROLE You'll work on real problems from your first week: the AI and data systems behind Healthcare Datahub and our work with frontier AI teams. You'll have a named mentor, and you'll own projects end to end, from scoping to shipped. WHAT YOU'LL WORK ON - Prototype and ship LLM features for Mira, our AI research assistant: dataset matching, cohort exploration and code generation. - Build data pipelines that turn raw healthcare datasets into documented, searchable, analysis-ready products. - Run experiments and evaluations, and present what you find at our Friday showcase. WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR - Currently pursuing a B.S., M.S. or Ph.D. in CS, AI/ML, data science, biomedical informatics or a related field. - Strong Python; experience with LLM APIs, PyTorch or Hugging Face. - Comfortable with SQL and messy real-world data. - Curious and self-directed; you'd rather ship something and learn than wait for a perfect spec. LOGISTICS - Spring, summer and fall terms; start dates are flexible. COMPENSATION $45 – $60 per hour, depending on degree level and experience.
