## Job description (verbatim)

WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH . This internship is part of the Apparel Materials Innovation team and will explore how computational methods can expand the discovery and understanding of apparel materials. You will collaborate with partners across Apparel Innovation, Digital, Global Apparel Materials, Design, Product Development, Advanced Manufacturing, testing, and data and analytics.. . WHO WE ARE LOOKING FO R. We are looking for a curious, analytical, and hands-on intern who is excited to work at the intersection of apparel materials, computational modeling, data science, and artificial intelligence. The successful candidate brings advanced academic training in materials, textiles, engineering, applied mathematics, data science, computer science, or a related field, plus the ability to frame technical questions, work with complex datasets, explore correlations, and communicate insights clearly.. . You are interested in modeling how fiber, yarn, textile structure, manufacturing process, finishing, and material properties influence testing, athlete, and product-performance outcomes. You are comfortable learning through ambiguity, working with imperfect or limited datasets, assessing the strength and limitations of correlations, and translating analysis into hypotheses and opportunities for materials discovery.. . You will work with guidance from experienced materials innovators and cross-functional partners. You communicate effectively with technical and nontechnical audiences, contribute reliably to shared work, and apply statistical analysis, computational modeling, machine learning, or AI-enabled approaches to uncover relationships, compare material systems, and inform experimentation and material decisions.. . Required qualifications. Currently pursuing a Master’s degree in materials science, textile engineering, mechanical engineering, applied mathematics, data science, computer science, statistics, computational science, or a related field. Will accept any suitable combination of education, experience and training.. Graduation date between Winter 2027 and Spring/Summer 2028.. Experience using quantitative methods to analyze experimental, material, engineering, or product-performance data through coursework, research, prior internships, or independent projects.. Working knowledge of programming and analytical tools such as Python, R, MATLAB, SQL, JMP, or comparable platforms, including data visualization and statistical analysis.. Familiarity with regression, multivariate analysis, design of experiments, uncertainty, model validation, or other methods used to evaluate relationships in complex datasets.. Strong problem-solving, organization, communication, and collaboration skills, with attention to data quality, reproducibility, and technical documentation.. . Preferred qualifications. Coursework or project experience in textiles, polymers, soft-matter mechanics, material characterization, apparel product creation, manufacturing, or performance testing.. Experience with machine learning, surrogate modeling, optimization, finite-element analysis, physics-informed methods, or generative AI applied to engineering or material problems.. Experience integrating datasets from multiple test methods or sources and developing features, metadata, taxonomies, or data pipelines for analysis.. Experience building computational notebooks, models, dashboards, or interactive visualizations that make technical relationships easier to interpret and use.. Interest in sport, athlete performance, sustainable materials, digital product creation, or computationally enabled materials discovery.. . . WHAT YOU’LL WORK ON . You will use computational and data-driven methods to investigate relationships across apparel material systems and surface opportunities for new material discovery. Your work will connect material composition and construction, manufacturing and finishing variables, laboratory testing, athlete insights, and product performance to develop models, hypotheses, and practical tools for apparel innovation teams.. You will aggregate, clean, and structure datasets covering fibers, yarns, textile constructions, manufacturing processes, finishes, physical properties, test results, athlete insights, and product outcomes.. You will define analytical features and improve metadata so that material variables and outcomes can be compared consistently across projects, test methods, and material systems.. You will develop exploratory and predictive models to identify correlations, interactions, clusters, tradeoffs, and potential performance drivers within apparel material data.. You will evaluate model quality and uncertainty, distinguish correlation from evidence of causation, and recommend targeted experiments or additional data needed to test promising hypotheses.. You will apply methods such as regression, multivariate analysis, dimensionality reduction, clustering, design of experiments, machine learning, or optimization to uncover opportunities for material discovery.. You will create reproducible computational workflows, visualizations, and technical documentation that allow materials innovators to interpret findings and build on the work.. You will share findings with materials, design, development, testing, manufacturing, and digital partners and translate model outputs into clear material hypotheses, experiment plans, and discovery opportunities.. .
