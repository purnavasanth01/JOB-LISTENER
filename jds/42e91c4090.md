## Job description (verbatim)

Aether Field is offering a summer internship focused on turning logistics operations data into reporting and insights that improve productivity and reduce costs. The intern will also help the team adopt AI tools for analysis and reporting.. . Responsibilities. Collect, clean, and analyze datasets from logistics operations, including transportation, parcel shipping, distribution center productivity, inventory, and direct-to-consumer fulfillment costs. Build and automate recurring reporting - dashboards, monthly cost and KPI reports, and executive-ready summaries - so the team spends less time assembling data and more time acting on it. Identify trends, anomalies, and cost drivers (for example carrier rates and surcharges, labor productivity, inventory health) and turn them into data-backed recommendations. Map the team’s recurring analyses and manual workflows, prioritize where AI and automation add the most value, and pilot one or more solutions end to end. Prototype AI-assisted tools, such as draft commentary for monthly performance reports, anomaly flagging on freight and labor data, and summarization of operating documents and carrier agreements. Improve data readiness for analytics and AI: standardize metric definitions, document data sources (warehouse, transportation, carrier, and finance systems), and set up repeatable data pipelines. Document solutions, build how-to and prompt guides, train colleagues, and measure adoption and time saved so improvements outlast the internship. Present findings and recommendations to Global Logistics leadership and bring relevant AI and analytics practices from the industry to the team. . Skills.
