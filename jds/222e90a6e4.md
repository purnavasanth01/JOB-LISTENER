User Experience UX Research Intern . As a UX Research Intern, you will be responsible for supporting usability research and testing for Dell’s client PCs and assisting with multiple ongoing UX research initiatives. You will work with industrial designers, engineers, and product managers on our consumer and commercial laptops, contributing to user-centered design and product innovation while gaining a front-row view of how user insights shape the hardware people use every day.. You will: . Support usability research and testing for client PCs, including usability studies, interviews, and hands-on product evaluations. Assist with multiple ongoing UX research initiatives, from planning and fieldwork through synthesis. Analyze findings and communicate clear, actionable insights to design, engineering, and product partners through presentations and visual summaries. Contribute to user-centered design and product innovation by turning what you observe into ideas the team can act on. Use AI tools to experiment with new ways of working and streamline research workflows, with mentorship from experienced researchers and designers . Take the first step towards your dream career .
