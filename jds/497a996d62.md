## Job description (verbatim)

Note: The job is a remote job and is open to candidates in USA. Method is hiring a Product Management Resident for its 2027 Summer Residency Program. The Resident will work on a cross-functional team to research user needs, shape product strategy, and help build, test, and deliver digital products, using AI tools as part of the product process.. . Responsibilities. Turn research and stakeholder input into a clear product concept, then express it as journey maps, flows and clickable prototypes. Use AI to go from interviews to a testable prototype quickly, and bring the craft and judgment to know when the result is good enough to put in front of users. Work side by side with Design Residents on usability testing, and let what users do, not what a deck says, drive the next decision. Translate goals into well-formed requirements and user stories that engineers and AI coding tools can act on. Build the technical fluency to discuss APIs, data, CI/CD and architecture trade-offs, and to plan scope, dependencies and capacity, including work done by AI agents. Work with Engineering Residents to decide what to build, buy or skip, and to check what AI-assisted development produces. You will contribute to a real product backlog, run light user research, and help make sure your team builds the right thing for the right users. Research and strategy. Combine market analysis, stakeholder interviews and AI-assisted synthesis to uncover product opportunities and test your hypotheses with real users. Requirements and specs. Turn high-level goals and "fuzzy" concepts into precise requirements, user stories and context that both people and AI tools can build from. Rapid prototyping. Use AI-assisted tools to turn ideas into prototypes or working demos, and put them in front of users early. Agile delivery. Practice Agile principles with your team: backlog ownership, sprint rituals, and iterative, value-driven delivery. Roadmapping and prioritization. Build outcome-focused roadmaps that balance vision, technical feasibility and constraints, and prioritize with evidence. Verification and quality. Review AI-generated research, specs and code with a critical eye, and help define how the team will know the product works. Storytelling and presentation. Turn complex data into clear narratives that win alignment, and present your team's progress and recommendations. AI scouting. Explore where generative AI can speed up the team's workflow or unlock new value, and be honest about where it does not help. . Skills. A strong interest in technology, digital products and how businesses operate. An interest in product management and a basic understanding of PM methodology, with a passion for creating great experiences. Hands-on curiosity about AI tools: you have experimented with them for school, work or personal projects, and you can say what worked and what did not. Excellent written and verbal communication, and comfort presenting ideas clearly. A proactive, self-motivated approach, and eagerness to learn. Comfort working in a team and balancing multiple learning opportunities. Familiarity with the software development lifecycle (SDLC), Agile principles, AI development lifecycle (AIDLC). Experience creating simple stories, flows, wireframes or prototypes. Experience with, or interest in, design, data, or technical fields such as software engineering, which will help you in a hybrid path. You are currently pursuing or have recently completed a degree in business, economics, computer science, information systems, data science, human-computer interaction, design, cognitive science or psychology, or a related field. We also hire Residents from non-traditional backgrounds as part of our effort to support career transitioners. If you are passionate about product management and have invested in your learning in other ways, we would love to hear about it. . Benefits. Personalized, one-on-one guidance from dedicated senior experts within your specific discipline (e.g., Product, Engineering, Design)..
