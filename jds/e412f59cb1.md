## Job description (verbatim)

We are looking for a motivated Technology Intern to help build the systems that run our business. Our Technology team is in building mode. We’re replacing off-the-shelf software with an in-house solution, a platform built around how Pathlabs works, and putting AI to work across the company. This role is perfect for someone who wants to build real tools with AI that teams use every day. You’ll use AI coding tools like Claude Code to turn business requirements into working features, and you’ll be responsible for making sure what gets built is accurate, secure and useful. You’ll work directly with our Technology, Media Services, Support and Data teams.. . * This internship will be paid and can be used towards college credits at The University of Montana. . Key Responsibilities . Pair with members of the Technology team to plan, build, test and launch new work . Build new features for our internal operating system, from product requirement documents and conversations with stakeholders and subject-matter experts . Use AI coding tools to build and update features across our platforms . Build and improve AI agents and skills that automate work for teams across the company . Build and maintain internal apps on low-code platforms to support daily workflows . Build custom apps and tools outside of our core operating system when teams need something new . Review, test and check AI-generated work before it reaches users . Make sure what you build handles data accurately and securely . Investigate and fix bugs reported by the teams who use our tools . Gather feedback from users and turn it into improvements . Document what you build so others can use and maintain it . What We're Looking For . Essential Skills . Ability to break a business problem into clear, step-by-step requirements . Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail, especially when checking AI-generated work . Comfortable learning new tools and technical systems quickly . Clear communication skills for working with technical and non-technical teammates . Educational Background . Currently studying or recently graduated in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Data Science, Information Systems or a related field . Coding experience (Python, JavaScript/TypeScript or SQL) and familiarity with AI tools (Claude, LLM APIs, AI agents) are helpful but not required . The Right Mindset . Curiosity about how systems work and why they break . Ownership of quality: you test your work before calling it done . Healthy skepticism of AI output and a habit of verifying it . Interest in solving real problems for the people using your tools . Comfort asking questions and taking feedback from teammates . Interest in how the business runs so you can build tools that fit it . What You'll Gain . This internship offers hands-on engineering experience, including: . Building and launching features used daily by teams across the company . Real experience building with AI coding tools, agents and automation . Working alongside the Technology team and learning how they plan, build and ship . Exposure to the full build process, from requirements to testing to launch . Seeing how a business runs from the inside and how technology supports each team . Experience working with technical and non-technical stakeholders . A portfolio of real projects you can point to in future roles .
